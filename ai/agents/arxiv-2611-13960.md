# From Attack Surface to Defense Architecture: Compositional Security for Agentic Coding Harnesses

## Summary

arXiv:2611.13960v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: AI coding harnesses couple language models to shells, filesystems, and network sockets so that agents can resolve issues and run tests. That coupling turns the harness into an automated Living-off-the-Land (LotL) platform: once an adversary can inject instructions into retrieved content, dual-use binaries such as bash, curl, and find become the payload rather than a vulnerability to be separately exploited. We argue that security in agentic systems is not an emergent behavioral property of individual language models, but a structural property of the cognitive architecture and of the composition of permissions across interacting agents and humans. Static regex blacklists miss flag permutations and structural attacks, whereas LLM-as-a-Judge guardrails ask a second model to guess physical invariants at tens to hundreds of milliseconds per call while over-blocking benign work.
 We present ActiveSecHarness, a neurosymbolic reference-monitor architecture organized around Separation of Intelligence and Authority across four pillars: (1) Macaroon-inspired HMAC capability tokens across four Trust and Authority Rings; (2) a product taint lattice L_trust x L_sens and a compositional Separation-of-Duties ledger over shared workspace provenance; (3) deterministic OS/subprocess guards; and (4) extended bytecode certificates, split-horizon dual-key audit logs, a self-healing verification loop (CodeVerifier, DualModelReviewer, SecurityTestSynthesizer), and a progressive pedagogical UserWikiManager. On N=252 fixtures and a 12+12 multi-step trajectory suite, Ring 0 achieves FPR=0.000 (0.939 unfitted out-of-sample / 0.962 OS-inert; 1.000 post-audit conformance ABR) in 31.5 us median latency (4,517x faster than a live local LLM-judge) and blocks 12/12 multi-step kill chains at 0.000 benign trajectory FPR (266.7 us/step; ~24 us/step O(1) ledger state folding).

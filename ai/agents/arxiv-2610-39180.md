# EmbodiExp: Compositional Experience Transfer for Embodied Agents

## Summary

arXiv:2610.39180v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Experience from past tasks can help embodied agents perform new tasks, but it may not remain applicable or effective when conditions change. Tasks with different overall goals often share subgoals, providing a basis for cross-task experience transfer. We present EmbodiExp, a framework for constructing outcome-grounded subgoal experiences and composing them in new tasks. Each experience describes how to pursue a subgoal, when the guidance applies, and what outcome must be verified for completion. Verified outcomes from applying one experience can satisfy the conditions for applying another, allowing experiences from different past tasks to be composed online. By examining the actions and observed outcomes at the subgoal level, EmbodiExp retains experience from successful subgoals and uses explicit action failures to identify actions to avoid under specific conditions. Experiments on EmbodiedBench and VIGIL assess cross-task transfer with experiences kept fixed throughout held-out evaluation. EmbodiExp achieves success rates of 68.0% on EB-Habitat and 68.3% on EB-ALFRED, compared with 50.0% and 54.7% for the agent without stored experience.

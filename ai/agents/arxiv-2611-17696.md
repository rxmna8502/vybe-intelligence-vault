# Co-Sight Harness: Externalized Control Assets and Deterministic Enforcement for Long-Horizon Business Agents

## Summary

arXiv:2611.17696v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Long-horizon business workflows fail in two ways: errors accumulate as a chain of activities lengthens, and the knowledge an activity needs is written in a different vocabulary from the user's request. We study whether such workflows can be made reliable without changing the language model. Our method keeps the backbone fixed and moves the workflow definition, state and domain knowledge into versioned control assets outside the model. A state machine and machine-checkable acceptance gates enforce these assets during execution; an independent auditor grades the resulting trace. The assets are updated only after a regression check and human approval .We evaluate the method on three deployed workflows exceeding 200 steps. On Domain A, a 120-case end-to-end evaluation set shows unattended completion rising from 50% to 100%, retrieval recall from 56% to 91.67%, and end-to-end latency dropping from 440 to 159 minutes, with scenario-analysis recall rising from 45% to 93%. Two other domains were taken over by teams that did not write the assets, one in four days and one in seven days. A controlled ablation separates the two failure sources. The code is available at https://github.com/ZTE-AICloud/Co-Sight/tree/master/Co-Sight%20Harness.

# Long-horizon autoformalization of a core theorem underlying MIP* = RE

## Summary

arXiv:2609.19814v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Landmark mathematical formalizations have taken specialist teams years to complete. We present FormalFlow, a system that coordinates AI proving agents under human supervision to address statement drift and proof composition in long-horizon formalization. Drawing on software-engineering principles and practices, it uses a shared blueprint to guide nested planning, proving, and review loops. Using FormalFlow, we completed a machine-checked Lean 4 proof of the quantum soundness of the classical low individual-degree test, a core theorem underlying MIP* = RE, in 63 days. The final library contains 126,367 lines of Lean code, all generated by agents. Beyond providing a verified foundation for quantum complexity, the formalization corrects side conditions and intermediate error bounds in the published proof and improves the error exponent of the main theorem. The development record shows what it takes to trust proofs written by AI agents and provides reusable tools for verifying research mathematics at scale.

# DSABench: Benchmarking Source Discovery, Coordination, and Execution for Multi-Database Agents

## Summary

arXiv:2610.14700v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Most text-to-SQL benchmarks provide the target database. In a large data catalog, however, choosing the sources is part of the problem: an agent must find every database needed for a request, determine how their results fit together, and execute the resulting workflow. We present DSABench to evaluate these capabilities jointly. Its 815 human-reviewed tasks draw on Spider, BIRD, and LiveSQLBench; 618 require several databases to contribute, and 105 allow more than one interpretation of the required sources. Executable contracts define source participation, cross-source dependencies, and source-local execution, while RouteEnv lets agents inspect public catalog evidence without revealing the gold annotations. In our routing-only study across seven model backends, the highest RA-Set score is 60.55%, compared with a highest RA-Graph score of 34.66% when coordination requirements are included. The gap becomes more pronounced when execution is required. Across four backends, median non-privileged Route+Exec-Graph success ranges from 6.69% to 7.98% without clarification and from 11.44% to 13.25% with one clarification; the best observed clarified configuration reaches 16.99%. On ambiguous tasks, clarification improves exact branch-source recovery by 39.8-43.2 percentage points. These findings illustrate why retrieving plausible databases or producing correct local SQL is not enough: an agent must carry the right information through the entire multi-database workflow.

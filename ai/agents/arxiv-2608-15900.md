# Crystal-structure design by agentic AI in a language of motifs

## Summary

arXiv:2608.15900v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Data-driven materials discovery interpolates more reliably than it extrapolates and seldom reveals new structure types. We present MatEvolve, an agentic-AI framework that designs crystals in an interpretable \emph{language of motifs}, in which each crystal is written as a \emph{motif profile} that describes the recurring geometric patterns---the \emph{motifs}---composing the crystal. The motif profile serves not merely as a description of a material but as the medium for material design: the agent edits the profile with a stated rationale and constructs a crystal from the modified one; the most promising candidates are then validated by first-principles calculations. MatEvolve is built on the state-of-the-art language model Claude Fable~5 without fine-tuning. It is applied to the design of rare-earth-lean permanent-magnet compounds and finds novel (meta)stable crystals that are both new structural prototypes and magnet compounds; under an equal validation budget, it does so an order of magnitude more often than generative models. Beyond design, analyzing the human-readable profiles of the discovered crystals reveals their structure--property relationships.

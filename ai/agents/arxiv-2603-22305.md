# Benchmarking LLM-Based Investment Advisor Agents for Asset Allocation in the Chinese ETF Market: Overview of NLPCC 2026 Shared Task 4

## Summary

arXiv:2603.22305v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) convert unstructured financial news into actionable trading strategies, yet standardized end-to-end benchmarks tailored for Chinese market remain scarce. We select common Chinese Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) as the investment universe and introduce CN-Buzz2Portfolio, the official benchmark of NLPCC 2026 Shared Task 4. Investment agents take daily trending Chinese financial news and historical ETF price-volume series as inputs to complete news filtering, news-to-ETF mapping and risk-controlled portfolio construction. To facilitate fair comparison, we provide a reproducible Compression--Perception--Allocation baseline compatible with mainstream LLMs. The task contains two ETF allocation tracks: Macro-Asset Allocation and Sector-Rotation Allocation. We adopt chronological data split across 2024--2026 to alleviate overfitting, with final rankings determined by out-of-sample Sharpe ratios on unseen test data. 26 teams registered for the task, and 14 submitted self-designed agent systems. Three core findings emerge: (1) Native LLM reasoning capability cannot translate to superior investment returns, as models show highly differentiated risk-return characteristics; (2) Hybrid frameworks are widely adopted, where LLMs extract textual semantic signals and quantitative modules incorporate price-volume factors and risk constraints; (3) Systems based on identical base LLMs yield distinct results with different signal processing, memory and portfolio construction modules. The project is publicly released at https://github.com/splash-li/NLPCC2026-Shared-Task-4.

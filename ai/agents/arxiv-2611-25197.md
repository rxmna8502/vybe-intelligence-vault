# From Self-Play RL to Constrained Control: Distilling DiffMPC Students with DAgger

## Summary

arXiv:2611.25197v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Self-play reinforcement learning (RL) produces strong driving policies, but these policies are hard to verify or interpret. Differentiable model predictive control (DiffMPC) can enforce explicit constraints, but it is usually trained by imitating logged data or with costly RL. To add constraints to an already trained policy, we keep a PPO teacher's backbone and replace its action head with a DiffMPC that enforces road boundary half-planes and agent ellipses. We then distill the student with DAgger in the teacher's self-play simulator. Over 9 seeds, the constrained student of a smaller teacher matches its teacher on nuPlan and beats it on CARLA and on out-of-distribution Waymo scenes. For a stronger teacher, the student has fewer failures on nuPlan and Waymo, while staying level on CARLA. Its distillation takes no more than 30% of the teacher's training time.

# Soft Decentralized Expected SARSA: Equilibrium Selection via Gradual Adaptation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33228v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We introduce Soft Decentralized Expected SARSA (SDES), a modification of Decentralized Q-learning in which agents maintain persistent exploration and update their acting Q-values gradually rather than switching directly to newly learned best responses when updating. We prove convergence to $\epsilon$-greedy Nash equilibria in weakly acyclic stochastic games for sufficiently large batch sizes. We then show, in the repeated Prisoner's Dilemma with memory, that soft updating can reshape basins of attraction without changing the equilibrium set. In the deterministic limit, we derive analytical basin bounds governed by the Q-value differences between actions in the target policies and show that reducing the policy learning rate can favor either cooperative or competitive equilibria. These results suggest that soft updating is not merely a stabilization device but also a mechanism of equilibrium selection in decentralized learning.

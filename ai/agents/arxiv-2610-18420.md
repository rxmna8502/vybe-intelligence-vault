# Agaram 2.5B: The First Tamil-First Language Model Trained from Scratch with Tool Calling. A Playbook for Under-Represented Languages

## Summary

arXiv:2610.18420v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We present Agaram 2.5B, a Tamil-first language model trained from scratch that converses in Tamil script, romanized Tanglish and English, and calls tools. Our first model, Agaram 1.5B, guided the new base, tokenizer and fine-tuning recipe: we pretrained a new 2.53B-parameter base on 50 billion tokens, 58% of them Tamil, with a tokenizer that needs 1.55 tokens per Tamil word where Qwen2.5's needs 9.44. A four-day public preview of our first 2.5B chat model showed where it fell short with real users, and we rebuilt supervised fine-tuning around what we saw, on 222,662 records. The released model scores above Sarvam-1 in all six translation directions we measured (FLORES chrF 56.9 into Tamil and 55.4 into English), chooses the expected tool on all 25 prompts of a tool set that was used during checkpoint selection and whose schemas appear in training, with exact arguments on 16, and was preferred over the preview model by an LLM judge on 100 sealed human-written prompts (net preference +30 points, 95% interval +14.5 to +45.0). On FLORES it also scores above Google's Gemma 4 E4B-it and our fine-tuned variant of it in both directions, with 2.53B parameters against E4B's 4.5B effective parameters. Factual knowledge and reliable refusal of harmful requests remain open problems, which we set out as future work. The base and chat weights and GGUF files are released under Apache-2.0, and this report gives a full account of the data, training and evaluation as a playbook for building language models for under-represented languages.

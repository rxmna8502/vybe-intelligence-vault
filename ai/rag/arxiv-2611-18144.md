# STAMP: Manufacturing-Informed Generative Design and Shape Optimization of Sheet Panels

## Summary

arXiv:2611.18144v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Generative design of formed sheet panels must preserve geometric manufacturing conditions while exploring layouts and improving performance. STAMP embeds these conditions directly in the shape representation. Each panel is described by its material layout and a reference surface with one height at each occupied position in a plane perpendicular to the drawing direction. Nominal thickness is prescribed along the normal. The representation allows curved surfaces, ribs, and openings. It excludes reference-surface undercuts and keeps nominal thickness fixed without manufacturing penalty terms. Because these conditions belong to the representation, every generated and optimized panel satisfies them regardless of training-set size. Boundary-conditioned diffusion generates alternative layouts, and predictor gradients guide a surface optimization that retains outlines, openings, and designated fixed regions. We demonstrate STAMP on automotive hood inner panels using CarHoods10k. With a comparable number of stored values, depth maps reconstruct surfaces more accurately than point-cloud and voxel baselines. From only five training designs, one of three training runs yields novel and varied layouts. A generator trained on 500 designs, 6.5% of the training set, already reaches the layout variety of the full-data model. Across 11 held-out design families, predictor-based selection among existing dataset designs yields an average gap of 0.18 mm to the best available FEA deformation, versus an expected 1.59 mm for random selection. It does better than random selection in all 11 families. At a 10% depth-change bound, a network not used in the search predicts an average deformation reduction of 24.8% for six generated and existing panels, with a 3.2% average mass increase computed from geometry. Engineers can thus explore and optimize panels within this manufacturing-informed design space.

# Embodied-VGGT: Preserving Reliable Local Geometry for Long-Sequence Reconstruction

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33964v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Long sequence reconstruction must correct accumulated drift while preserving locally consistent geometry. In robotics and autonomous driving, embodied acquisition provides recurring platform- environment geometry and repeated observations of scene structure and platform motion. We present Embodied-VGGT, which exploits these cues to establish reliable local relationships and selectively preserve them during global refinement. Memory-Augmented Grounded Chunks (MAGC) builds on Scal3R's global context memory and uses relative scale references derived from predicted ground geometry to regularize test-time memory updates, encouraging cross-chunk consistency while limiting changes to reliable local geometry. Superpoint Alignment (SA) and Cross-Loop Optimization (CL) jointly address the robust establishment and preservation of geometric relationships. SA balances ground and non-ground evidence across spatial regions to estimate inter-chunk translation. CL uses geometric and motion consistency across interleaved windows to select coherent chunk groups, each receiving a shared Sim(3) correction that preserves its internal relative transformations during global optimization. Experiments on five public driving benchmarks demonstrate consistent and substantial improvements in long sequence reconstruction. On KITTI Odometry, Embodied-VGGT achieves a mean absolute trajectory error of 7.30 m and a Chamfer distance of 2.67 m, reducing these errors by 46.8% and 23.3% compared with the state-of-the-art baseline under same evaluation protocol. To facilitate reproducibility, we released our code: https://github.com/Ang-Sheep/Embodied-VGGT.

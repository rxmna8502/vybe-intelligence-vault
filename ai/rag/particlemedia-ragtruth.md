# ParticleMedia/RAGTruth

## Summary

*   Introduces RAGTruth, a specialized corpus designed to identify and quantify hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models.
*   Facilitates the development of trustworthy RAG systems by providing a benchmark for evaluating factual consistency and mitigating generative inaccuracies.
*   Offers a dataset for fine-tuning and robust evaluation of RAG architectures against hallucination generation, crucial for improving model reliability.

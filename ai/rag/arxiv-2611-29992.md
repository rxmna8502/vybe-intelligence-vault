# Sampling with graph wavelet kernels: optimality and adaptivity to regularity and geometry

## Summary

arXiv:2611.29992v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We construct wavelet-based determinantal samplers on manifolds and geometric graphs whose approximation rates adapt to the regularity of the observable. On a Riemannian manifold of dimension $m$, our wavelet DPPs satisfy variance reduction of order $n^{1-2s/m}$ for linear statistics of both $s$-H\"older and $H^s$ observables, where $0<s\le1$. Using these samplers, we further construct unbiased quadrature rules with root mean square error $O(n^{-1/2-s/m})$ using at most $3n/2$ function evaluations, matching the classical optimal randomized integration exponent. For point clouds sampled from an $m$-dimensional manifold, we develop corresponding determinantal samplers on two standard classes of geometric random graphs: $\varepsilon$-graphs and symmetric $k$-nearest-neighbour graphs. These samplers satisfy the same variance reduction for linear statistics of $s$-H\"older observables and yield conditionally unbiased estimators of empirical averages with root mean square error $O(n^{-1/2-s/m})$, using at most $2n$ function evaluations. In both settings, the rates are governed by the intrinsic dimension $m$ and the regularity $s$ of the observable.

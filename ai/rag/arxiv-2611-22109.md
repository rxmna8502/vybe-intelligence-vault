# VTT: Reading Vehicle Size and Powertrain Type from Motion Trajectory Across Unseen Car Models

## Summary

arXiv:2611.22109v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Vehicle size and powertrain information can enrich trajectory data for traffic analysis and simulation. We present VTT, a dataset and benchmark for identifying these attributes from motion and controller area network (CAN) signals across unseen car models. The dataset contains 9,062 drives, about 3,950 h, from 1,431 vehicles and 174 car models. It provides three footprint-based size classes, powertrain labels, and evaluation on unseen car models and unseen drivers. Using 60-s windows, we compare four sequence architectures, statistical features and physical features proposed by large language models. Training with auxiliary physical targets, data augmentation and task-specific weighting raises drive-level macro-F1 to 92.6-93.0% for battery-electric versus combustion vehicles and from 62.9% to 67.1% for size classification. On public logs from 4,061 additional drivers, the full-input model identifies powertrain correctly for 86 of 88 car models; size accuracy is 63.2%, close to the internal vehicle-level result of 63.5%. Physical-feature analysis links powertrain recognition to deceleration behaviour and size recognition to steering response. Errors near size-class boundaries and limited gains from longer observations highlight the importance of label resolution and training-fleet coverage for cross-model generalisation.

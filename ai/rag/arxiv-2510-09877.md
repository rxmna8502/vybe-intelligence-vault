# Batch Bayesian Active Learning with Partial Batch Label Sampling

## Summary

arXiv:2510.09877v4 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Over the past couple of decades, many active learning acquisition functions have been proposed, leaving practitioners with an unclear choice of which to use. Bayesian active learning offers principled objectives with explainable intuition, including Expected Error Reduction (EER), Expected Predictive Information Gain (EPIG), and Bayesian Active Learning by Disagreements (BALD). A key challenge of such methods is the difficulty of extending to the batch setting, leading to either computational challenges (BatchBALD) or dramatic performance drops (top-$B$ selection). In this work, we extend active learning's Bayesian Decision Theory framework to derive Partial Batch Label Sampling (ParBaLS) for the EPIG algorithm. We show experimentally that ParBaLS EPIG gives superior performance for a variety of datasets with different budgets using Bayesian logistic regression on embeddings from large pre-trained models. Our code is available at https://github.com/ADDAPT-ML/ParBaLS.

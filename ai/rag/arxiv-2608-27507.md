# Learning Complementary Routes for Scouting Persimmon Orchards during Shoot Growth with CoverShare

## Summary

arXiv:2608.27507v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: At the study orchard, scouting during persimmon shoot growth focuses on leaf color change, leaf area, and internode length. Several routes may share an access lane, yet each can offer a view of row segments that the others miss. We introduce CoverShare to learn complementary routes under a fixed action budget. Within Policy Gradient for Parallel State Entropy maximization (PGPSE), CoverShare redistributes auxiliary rewards according to the state coverage lost when each policy is omitted and the number of policies visiting each state. It preserves the pooled entropy objective and total auxiliary reward. We simulated the annotated road network of a 6.7-ha site with 107 observable persimmon scouting units across five zones. At 40 actions per route, six distinct policies covered an expected 17.84 units perinspection, compared with 8.28 for six independent runs of one policy, averaged across eight training seeds. With the same policies trained on 40-action routes, extending each inspection route to 80 and 120 actions raised coverage to 22.52 and 28.38 units. Across seven public discrete-state tasks, CoverShare increased state support by a mean of 16.29% over the team-entropy baseline. Controlled ablations identified leave-one-policy-out coverage as the main contributor to these gains.

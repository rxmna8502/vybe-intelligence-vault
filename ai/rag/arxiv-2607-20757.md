# GaugeQuant: Online Learning of Quantization-Optimal Bases from LLM Symmetries

## Summary

arXiv:2607.20757v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Transformers are known to have internal continuous symmetries that leave outputs invariant while altering how activations are distributed across coordinates, and hence how well they quantize. GaugeQuant leverages this in-training by introducing a LogSumExp term to the loss that breaks the symmetries, thus selecting a basis that minimizes activation outliers. A stop-gradient operator ensures that only rotation matrices are updated, yielding the language modeling objective completely unaltered. Our quantization strategy requires no specific calibration data, no quantization simulation, and adds a small inference overhead. As a proof of concept, we evaluate on WikiText-2 perplexity and zero-shot reasoning. On Qwen-2.5 0.5B, GaugeQuant reduces perplexity (e.g. $25.62 \to 16.76$ under W4A16) and improves zero-shot accuracy. On LLaMA-2 7B, perplexity improves but downstream accuracy does not. The code is available at: https://github.com/MPedraBento/gauge-quant.

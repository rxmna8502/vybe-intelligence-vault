# ENACT-Heart -- ENsemble-based Assessment Using CNN and Transformer on Heart Sounds

## Summary

arXiv:2502.16914v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: ENACT-Heart is a two-branch ensemble for heart-sound classification. A Vision Transformer (ViT) processes spectrogram images, while a convolutional neural network (CNN) processes visualizations of waveforms and spectral centroids derived from the same recordings. The branch predictions are combined by a weighted average with fixed, input-independent weights. On the PASCAL heart-sound dataset, the ensemble achieved an accuracy of 97.52%, compared with 93.88% for the ViT and 95.45% for the CNN. The ensemble achieved a macro-F1 of 0.94 and recall of 0.74 for extra heart sounds. On this dataset, late fusion improved accuracy and macro-F1 relative to both individual branches.

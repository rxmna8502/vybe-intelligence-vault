# From Chats to Markets: AgenticPay for LLM-Powered Negotiation in Multi-Agent Commerce

## Summary

arXiv:2602.06008v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Large language model agents are increasingly expected to negotiate and transact autonomously, yet existing benchmarks rarely combine multimodal product grounding, multi-issue bargaining, and competitive multi-agent markets. We introduce AgenticPay, a unified benchmark for evaluating multimodal agents' ability to reach high-welfare, multidimensional agreements in realistic markets. AgenticPay includes 160 multimodal tasks across four business scenarios (E-commerce, Food Delivery, Ride-hailing, and Apartment Rental) and eight market topologies, from bilateral bargaining to many-to-many competition. Agents interpret product images, infer opponents' hidden preferences, and negotiate binding contract terms such as price, lease duration, return policy, and delivery speed through multi-round natural-language dialogue. A utility-based framework evaluates agreement quality and joint welfare. Experiments with proprietary (GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview) and open-weight (Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct, InternVL3-38B) multimodal models reveal substantial limitations in value creation and market reasoning. Even the strongest model, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, achieves a GlobalScore of only 42.3/100. As markets shift from bilateral to multi-sided, average GlobalScore falls by 5.7 points, with open-weight models declining by up to 11.5 points. AgenticPay provides a testbed for evaluating multimodal agents in realistic commerce. Code and data: https://github.com/SAIL-Research-Lab/AgenticPay.

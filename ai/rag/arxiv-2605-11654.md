# Skypart : Weather-Robust Cross-View Geo-Localization via Prototype-Based Semantic Part Discovery

## Summary

arXiv:2605.11654v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: The low-altitude economy is growing rapidly, increasing demand for reliable drone navigation. Common tasks such as delivery, inspection, and search-and-rescue rely on Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), which can be jammed, spoofed, or unavailable. Cross-view geo-localization (CVGL), which matches an oblique drone view to a geo-referenced satellite tile, offers an alternative for autonomous localization when GNSS fails. However, current CVGL methods have three limitations: (1) global descriptors compress patch grids into a single vector, without separating cross-view-shared layout from view-specific texture; (2) altitude-related scale variation remains in the learned embedding instead of being marginalized as a nuisance; and (3) multi-objective training uses hand-tuned loss weights despite incompatible gradient scales. We propose SKYPART, a lightweight, swappable head for patch-based vision transformers (ViTs) that explicitly groups patches into parts. It combines (i) learnable prototypes that compete for patch tokens through single-pass cosine assignment; (ii) altitude-conditioned linear modulation used only during training, yielding altitude-free retrieval embeddings at inference; (iii) graph-attention readout over active prototypes; and (iv) Kendall uncertainty-weighted multi-objective learning that learns loss-group weights end-to-end. With 26.95M parameters and 22.14 GFLOPs, SKYPART is the smallest top-performing method in our comparison and achieves state-of-the-art results on SUES-200, University-1652, and DenseUAV using single-pass retrieval without reranking or test-time augmentation (TTA). Its accuracy advantage over the strongest baseline further increases under ten WeatherPrompt corruption conditions. Code: https://github.com/chisngyen/SkyPart.

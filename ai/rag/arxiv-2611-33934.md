# Towards 360$^\circ$ Embodied UAV Tracking Without Turning

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33934v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Conventional embodied UAV tracking couples target observability with heading control because targets may leave the field of view of forward-facing cameras. We investigate a complementary paradigm that uses full-spherical perception to follow designated targets without active heading adjustment. To support this setting, we introduce 360UAV-ETrack, a simulation benchmark comprising 2,652 sequences, over 1.73 million synchronized observations, eight tracking tasks, and 150 held-out closed-loop scenarios. We propose 360UAV-TVLA, a panoramic vision--language--action (VLA) framework integrating persistent visual referring (PVR), compressed temporal memory (CTM), and geometry-aligned trajectory supervision (GTS) for instance identification, motion reasoning, and three-dimensional control. Experiments across two VLA backbones show consistent gains over standard fine-tuning, with normalized average tracking frames increasing from 22.56\% to 35.28\% for $\pi_0$ and from 25.68\% to 33.64\% for $\pi_{0.5}$. Our benchmark further reveals a substantial gap between offline imitation accuracy and closed-loop tracking reliability, establishing a challenging testbed for future research https://libingyu01.github.io/360UAV-ETrack_360UAV-TVLA/

# From Classifier Heads to Encoder Prototypes: Personalized Federated Learning with Head-Derived Clustering

## Summary

arXiv:2610.30075v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Personalized federated learning must decide which clients should share representations. We introduce HDC-FL (Head-Derived Clustering for Personalized Federated Learning), which reads that decision off a signal shared-encoder training already produces: the client-specific classifi- er heads. At periodic refresh rounds the server projects the cached heads into a normalized low-rank descriptor space, groups the descriptors, builds one encoder prototype per group from the cached client encoders, and returns to each client a soft mixture of those prototypes paired with its own unchanged head. Client-specific heads are never averaged or distributed to oth- er clients; the shared-encoder trajectory is left intact; and no second optimization trajectory, additional local objective, or post-training fine-tuning stage is required. Across 11 CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 configurations covering label-subset, Dirichlet and rotation heterogeneity with ResNet-18 and MobileNetV2 encoders, HDC-FL is competitive with the strongest personalized and clustered baselines in macro-averaged mean accuracy and attains the numerically highest macro-averaged worst-client and bottom-20% accuracy among the evaluated methods. The tail results distinguish methods that look similar when only their means are compared. A controlled donor-swap diagnostic further finds positive donor-ranking associations between head similarity and encoder compatibility in all three settings tested, most clearly under covariate shift.

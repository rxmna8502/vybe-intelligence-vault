# Efficient probabilistic weather forecasting at quarter-degree resolution with two-sided transport attention

## Summary

arXiv:2610.35545v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: High-resolution probabilistic weather forecasting remains computationally demanding. Here we present Astra, a 0.25$^\circ$ ensemble model trained on eight H200 GPUs. It combines Kantorovich Attention, a two-sided transport attention in which each region chooses where it reads from and writes to, so every region sends and receives at token resolution, with a self-featurised continuous ranked probability score (CRPS) that also scores forecasts in the model's multiscale features. In WeatherBench 2, Astra matches GenCast's CRPS averaged over five upper-air targets and days 1-10 with 9.8% of its hardware-normalised training compute. Verified against ERA5, its CRPS and ensemble-mean root-mean-square error are lower than the ECMWF ensemble's on all 600 variable, level and lead-time combinations, and its CRPS is lower than GenCast's on 73.5% of them. A 15-day member needs 39 times fewer floating-point operations than GenCast's GPU implementation, and 50 members take under 40 min on one consumer GPU, within 21 GB.

# PointEvent: Polarity-Conditioned Motion Evidence Accumulation for Event-Based Tiny Object Detection

## Summary

arXiv:2609.20066v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Event cameras offer high temporal resolution and motion sensitivity for tiny UAV detection, but distant targets produce sparse, fragmented events easily overwhelmed by clutter and noise. Dense aggregation mixes weak target responses with background activity, while local spatiotemporal modeling may fail to connect distant observations. Polarity encodes the sign of brightness change; a moving target tends to produce opposite polarities at its leading and trailing boundaries, whose spatial arrangement indicates motion direction. We propose PointEvent, which serializes events into complementary spatiotemporal and timestamp orders. Spatiotemporal orders preserve x-y-t locality, while timestamp orders preserve temporal succession across spatial neighborhoods, establishing long-range propagation paths. Along each path, a recurrent state accumulates evidence, while polarity routes each event to its corresponding state subspace. This separates how evidence propagates from where it is written. Experiments on EV-UAV and ES-UAV demonstrate state-of-the-art accuracy with the fewest parameters (0.12M) and fastest measured inference (29.9 ms) among compared methods. Code: https://github.com/wzz-z/PointEvent

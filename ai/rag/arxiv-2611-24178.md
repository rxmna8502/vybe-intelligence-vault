# Adaptive Action Horizon Selection via Flow-Field Uncertainty

## Summary

arXiv:2611.24178v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Reliable uncertainty estimates are important for adaptive execution in vision-language-action (VLA) models. Existing approaches such as Adaptive Action Chunking (AAC) estimate action entropy from multiple sampled action chunks, introducing additional inference cost and capturing both uncertainty within an action trajectory and diversity across alternative strategies. We introduce Divergence-based Uncertainty-aware Adaptive Action Chunking (DUAC), a post-hoc method that extracts trajectory-conditioned predictive uncertainty from the vector field of a pretrained VLA. Building on a closed-form flow-posterior covariance formulation, DUAC derives action-level uncertainty scores from a single generated chunk using Jacobian-vector products, without retraining or multiple endpoint samples. DUAC strongly correlates with AAC, while capturing distinct information: AAC measures global dispersion across sampled chunks, whereas DUAC measures local uncertainty around the trajectory selected for execution. Consequently, DUAC is less sensitive to diversity among distinct but valid action strategies. Across LIBERO, LIBERO-Pro, LIBERO-Plus, and RoboCasa, DUAC-based adaptive horizon selection performs favorably among the evaluated methods, combining strong task success with low uncertainty-estimation latency. These results establish the flow vector field as an efficient, trajectory-conditioned source of predictive uncertainty for robot decision making.

# Callo: Serving Cache-Miss Requests with Speculative Decoding on Invalidated KV under Prefill-Decode Disaggregation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.18459v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Agent sessions may involve frequent context edits, which effectively invalidate cached KV state and force requests to wait for re-prefill before decoding. We present Callo, a disaggregated serving system that leverages invalidated KV for speculative drafts to reduce this latency, while verifying drafts against the exact KV after prefill before committing tokens. Callo makes effective use of decode resources by scheduling speculations during periods of underutilization and preempting speculations when drafts are likely to not be accepted. We evaluated Callo on a two-GPU testbed using SWE-agent sessions across three model families, observing all-request speedups of 1.05-1.19x over vLLM at moderate load. We demonstrate that these gains are also realized at scale through cluster-level simulations driven by real-world traces.

# Cross-Protocol Personality Assessment with LLMs: Aligning Behavioral Evidence with Human Ratings

## Summary

arXiv:2610.16050v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Personality assessment from asynchronous video interviews (AVIs) plays a crucial role in recruitment by enabling standardized and behaviorally grounded evaluation for job applicants. However, most of the previous works are confined to a single interview protocol. Independently collected protocols differ in question sets and question-trait mapping. Thus, the human-rated evidence from one protocol cannot be used directly under another. To address this limitation, we propose the Cross-protocol Evidence-grounded Personality Assessment (CEPA) framework, which aligns behavioral representations across interview protocols. CEPA addresses protocol heterogeneity by projecting source behavioral records into the target protocol's construct-oriented representation space. It retrieves behaviorally relevant and personality-complementary human-rated exemplars to ground an ensemble of LLM raters, which enables behavior-to-personality associations without target-specific supervision. In cross-protocol experiments, CEPA reduces average mean squared error (MSE) by 43.8% (Forward) and 17.0% (Reverse) relative to the strongest baseline. Our empirical experiments show that human-rated evidence from one protocol can support personality assessment under another without target-specific supervision or retraining.

# Quantifying Local and Global Fidelity of Nonlinear Embeddings

## Summary

arXiv:2611.00290v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Dimension reduction rests on a claim: t-SNE and UMAP preserve local structure, PCA global structure. Our DLP curve is the share of each point's k nearest neighbors a map keeps. Corrected for chance, it gives L at k=10 and G over 30 to 50% of the data, local and global fidelity. At the midpoint the curve is provably a rank correlation of distances. The LG reversal test decides, with a stated error rate, which of two maps is local and which global. On twelve datasets of up to 1.38 million points, G agrees with established global scores at 0.84 and 0.93, and the area under R_NX at 0.22 and 0.30. The entity fidelity E, read at each point's entity size, predicts which entities a map loses beyond L and G where entity sizes differ. In an agent memory of one million passages, t-SNE and PCA cross at 150,000 passages (p=2.6e-5).

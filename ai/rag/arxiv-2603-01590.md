# IDProxy: CTR Prediction with Multimodal LLMs for Cold-Start Recommendation at Xiaohongshu

## Summary

arXiv:2603.01590v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Content-driven platforms such as Xiaohongshu often leverage click-through rate (CTR) prediction models for recommendation. However, these models depend heavily on item ID embeddings, which perform poorly in item cold-start settings. In this paper, we present IDProxy, a production-scale system developed at Xiaohongshu to address this challenge. IDProxy leverages multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to generate proxy embeddings from rich content signals, enabling CTR prediction for new items in the absence of usage data. Through a lightweight coarse-to-fine mechanism, these proxies are aligned with the ID embedding space and trained end-to-end with the ranking model, allowing seamless integration into production-facing pipelines. Extensive offline and online experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the method, which has been deployed in 2025 in Xiaohongshu's Content Feed and Display Ads features, reaching hundreds of millions of users daily.

# VETNET: Assembling Benchmark Datasets for AI-Driven Veterinary Innovation

## Summary

arXiv:2610.14149v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: VETNET represents an ecosystem of live benchmark datasets and evaluation frameworks designed to accelerate AI-driven innovation in animal science and veterinary medicine through standardized, high-quality datasets and multi-institutional collaboration across academic and non-academic sectors. This framework addresses the gap between AI advances in human medicine and the fragmented state of veterinary AI development.

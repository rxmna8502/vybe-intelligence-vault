# From Manufacturing Event Logs to Simulation: Enabling SMEs with Agentic AI for SimPy Simulation Generation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27483v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: High expertise and cost barriers exclude small and medium-sized enterprises from discrete-event simulation (DES). We introduce GenAI4SimPy, a multi-agent framework powered by Qwen 3.6 (27B), an open-weight large language model, that automates end-to-end DES generation from manufacturing event logs to executable SimPy models through tool-augmented reasoning and human-in-the-loop validation. Evaluated on three execution blocks from a public smart factory dataset, the framework achieved a mean absolute percentage error of 7.9\% and coverage of failure rate for all three blocks and of throughput for two of three. A fidelity paradox was observed: accurate process-level parameters did not ensure system-level behavioral fidelity, with flow-time and throughput errors driven by misinterpreted observation-window truncation and absent system-level calibration. These results demonstrate that local open-weight models can perform DES generation at accuracy comparable to cloud-based alternatives, while identifying parameter extraction as necessary but insufficient for behavioral validity. Source code and results are available at https://github.com/RaikoPipe/genai4simpy

# SkillPE: Creativity-Oriented Cinematic Skill Evolution for Text-to-Video Prompt Enhancement

## Summary

arXiv:2609.34335v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Achieving high-quality cinematic text-to-video generation remains challenging for non-experts, whose prompts often lack professional narrative and creative design. Existing prompt enhancement (PE) methods and static cinematic skills offer limited capabilities for exploring diverse creative realizations. We propose SkillPE, a creativity-oriented PE framework that systematically evolves reusable cinematic skills through reference-guided exploration. Starting from expert-provided cinematic knowledge, SkillPE initializes and normalizes 20 structured seed skills. It then retrieves movie references categorized as resonators (good matches), dissonants (weak matches), and divergents (creatively inspiring near-misses). Resonators and dissonants refine skill applicability and execution, while divergents inspire alternative cinematic realizations through controlled mutations that preserve user intent. Candidate skills are assessed through generated videos along prompt fidelity, cinematic quality, narrative appeal, and creativity to construct the final skill libraries. Experiments on StoryEval and VBench demonstrate improvements of up to 1.10 points over seed skills and 1.02 points over the strongest external baseline on a 7-point four-dimensional evaluation scale. Overall, SkillPE offers a practical approach to balancing fidelity and creativity in cinematic text-to-video generation.

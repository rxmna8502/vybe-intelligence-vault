# Denoising the US Census: Succinct Block Hierarchical Regression

## Summary

arXiv:2603.10099v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: The 2020 United States Census adopted differential privacy to protect individual confidentiality, adding calibrated noise to summary demographic measurements that are then reconciled into population tables. We developed BlueDown, a postprocessing algorithm that combines techniques from linear regression and constrained optimization while exploiting measurement structure for efficiency. On 2020 Census data, BlueDown reduced the error of groupings by race at the county level by 21-36% and of all county-level groupings by 9-58%; at the tract level, it reduced error of groupings by housing type by 38-61%. These gains came at no cost to confidentiality or computational efficiency, and would improve the validity of research relying on this definitive dataset and analyses used to guide the distribution of over $1.5 trillion in annual federal spending.

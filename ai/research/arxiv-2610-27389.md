# Fixed-Size Active Statistical Inference

## Summary

arXiv:2610.27389v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Variance reduction techniques improve the efficiency of drawing conclusions from data. Recent proposals for variance-reduction use two complementary techniques to reduce variance: incorporating machine learning predictions into estimators and weighted sampling. A popular weighted sampling algorithm is Poisson Sampling (PS), which independently flips a biased coin at each unit and includes all heads in the sample. A fundamental flaw with PS is that it has a random sample size, which presents practical challenges and introduces variance into the estimator. We show that a fixed-size alternative, known in survey sampling as Conditional Poisson Sampling (CPS), is a drop-in replacement for PS on general multivariate Z-estimation problems, which include convex M-estimation considered in prior work. Notably, we show that the asymptotic variance of CPS is no worse than that of PS, and can be substantially better in realistic scenarios where predictions are imperfect. We illustrate these advantages on both synthetic and real datasets, and release a Python package implementing CPS. In light of our contributions, there is no need to suffer a variable sample size.

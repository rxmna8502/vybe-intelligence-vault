# Convergence Properties of PF-Exec: Homogeneous Structure, Anchored Closed Form, and Gain Dynamics

## Summary

arXiv:2611.17488v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: This paper establishes a layered mathematical char acterisation of PF-Exec, the algorithm of the companion system paper. Demand-proportional bandwidth allocation yields an exact first-order homogeneous decomposition, from which the equal-load bandwidth target and its feasibility bound follow in closed form. The logarithmic knee view preserves strict load order and shares its global optimum with the Jain fairness index, while the demand-weighted centroid minimises the position potential. The damped softmax association block possesses a fixed point on the product of simplices. The truncated one-dimensional gain iteration admits a complete characterisation, with geomet ric convergence below the critical gain, initial-value-dependent bounded oscillation at it, and a family of bounded two-state cycles above it. A global invariant error ball and an explicit tracking recursion complete the execution guarantee. A numerical audit confirms the structural identities on every measured state and quantifies the local amplification of the coupled association and resource dynamics. Together, the results explain how closed form recovery directions and bounded adaptive gains provide stable execution without requiring a global Euclidean contraction assumption.

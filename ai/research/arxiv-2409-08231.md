# Design Optimization of Nuclear Fusion Reactor through Deep Reinforcement Learning

## Summary

arXiv:2409.08231v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: In this research, we investigate deep reinforcement learning (DRL) as a practical tool for constrained, multi-objective optimization for tokamak fusion reactor designs. The task is to optimize the design configurations that minimize building cost while achieving high energy gain and long energy confinement time, subject to the four operational limits required for stable operation. Due to the coupled physics-engineering evaluation, the resulting objectives and constraints are black-box, making it challenging to find optimal solutions with conventional search strategies. To address this, we implement Reward-Constrained Policy Optimization (RCPO) for tokamak fusion reactor design. This method integrates multiple constraints into the reward functions, guiding the policy towards feasible solutions and simplifying the design optimization process. Our numerical results show that the proposed DRL-based approach finds more feasible designs that satisfy operational constraints more efficiently than other data-driven optimization methods, demonstrating the potential of DRL for efficient future reactor design.

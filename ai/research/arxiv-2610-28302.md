# Learning Normal Coordinates on Riemannian Manifolds with Flow Charts

## Summary

arXiv:2610.28302v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Riemannian manifolds are powerful models for problems with non-Euclidean structure. Recent work in geometric machine learning has focused on training deep neural networks to learn Riemannian metrics from high-dimensional data. However, many geometric algorithms require repeated evaluations of the exponential and logarithmic maps, whose high computational cost restricts the utility of learned Riemannian manifolds in practice. We propose to address this cost by training a conditional invertible neural network, called a flow chart, to approximate normal coordinates around points on a given Riemannian manifold. With this approach, the exponential and logarithmic maps can be approximated by fast Euclidean operations in the model's learned coordinate space. Our main contribution is an efficient loss function for training flow charts that is nonnegative and, under suitable domain assumptions, is zero precisely when the chart is normal. Across sixteen synthetic and data-derived geometries, flow charts are competitive with a specialized neural geodesic interpolator for logarithmic map estimation, while also being able to estimate the exponential map. When pretrained, flow charts provide a median speedup of 60-70x over numerical baselines at comparable logarithmic map estimation error, and their learned coordinates improve the accuracy of a numerical geodesic solver.

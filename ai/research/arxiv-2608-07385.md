# Omni-modal decomposition autoencoders learn full-stack wearable disentangled representations

## Summary

arXiv:2608.07385v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Learning disentangled representations is key for general-purpose multi-modal wearable models, yet existing approaches rarely combine classification, disentanglement, fusion, and generation of heterogeneous multi-modal time series in a single model. We introduce Omni-modal Variational Decomposition Autoencoders (OmniDecVAEs), a framework that learns multi-purpose representations from arbitrarily many modalities with a single shared encoder-decoder. OmniDecVAEs extend DecVAEs by learning time-frequency latent subspaces per modality through a multi-view self-supervised decomposition loss and a component-wise conditional decoder. On the omni-modal human activity recognition (HAR) HARWE dataset (up to thirty channels) and the public UCI HAR and WESAD datasets, OmniDecVAEs learn the most disentangled representations for unseen subjects (DCI disentanglement up to 3.0 percentage points (pp) higher). On HARWE, they improve subject-dependent HAR and identity recognition by 1.1 pp and 5.8 pp, and outperform supervised end-to-end models by +31.6 pp and +22.3 pp on unseen subjects of multi-device datasets. Moreover, their generative capacity improves reconstruction mean squared error over multi-modal VAEs (34-74% improvement) and their synthetic data improve affect recognition on unseen subjects (2 pp). With 4.1M parameters, invariant to the number of modalities (21xfewer than a multi-branch VAE), OmniDecVAEs are scalable, single-model full-stack learners for wearable representations.

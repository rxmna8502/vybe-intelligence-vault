# EgoPhys: Physics-Informed Digital Twins for Deformable Objects from Egocentric Video

## Summary

arXiv:2606.16202v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Humans naturally understand object physics through everyday interactions, but predicting how deformable objects, such as soft objects and fabrics, respond to manipulation remains a major challenge for computer vision and robotics. Existing deformable physical twin methods typically rely on controlled multi-view or depth capture and costly per-object physical refinement. We present EgoPhys, a framework for constructing predictive deformable physical twins from a single egocentric RGB interaction. EgoPhys combines persistent correspondences with per-frame monocular 3D observations to fit a sequence-specific spring-mass model from moving-camera observations. Because a short interaction weakly constrains fine-grained local stiffness, EgoPhys amortizes this refinement across training interactions using a compact state-conditioned residual model that predicts bounded local corrections around the sequence-specific estimate and remains frozen at test time. Experiments show improved held-out future prediction over adapted deformable-twin baselines, with the amortized residual further improving prediction while reducing local-refinement cost by approximately 10-59x over dense per-spring optimization. The resulting twins also support deformable-object action selection, with representative physical robot manipulation providing a qualitative proof of concept.

# VASR: Vector-Aware Assembly Planning with Equivalent Structures and Repair-Cost Feedback

## Summary

arXiv:2611.20984v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Furniture assembly planning requires inferring subassembly hierarchies from individual parts and a target illustration. Existing raster-based methods do not explicitly exploit the geometric and structural information in SVG drawings. We introduce VASR, a framework that fuses features from a dedicated SVG encoder with visual representations from a pretrained vision-language model to predict assembly hierarchies. To avoid penalizing valid alternatives to a reference assembly tree, we propose Repair-Aware Hierarchical Policy Optimization (RHPO) with hierarchy-preserving equivalence rewards. RHPO further uses repair-cost feedback to favor decisions with lower downstream correction costs among candidates with comparable equivalence-aware rewards. VASR achieves the highest Equivalent-Hard F1 among the evaluated methods, outperforming GPT6-Astra by 17.69 percentage points on the IKEA-derived test set while maintaining strong performance on an independent furniture dataset.

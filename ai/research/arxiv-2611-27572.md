# Minimax-Optimal Small-Loss Regret in Online Convex Optimization and Applications to Contextual Search and Online Inverse Optimization

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27572v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We give small-loss regret bounds for online convex optimization (OCO), using one subgradient per round. Let $\Delta_T^\star$ denote the cumulative loss of the best fixed decision in hindsight, measured relative to the per-round minima. Our algorithm achieves the regret bound $O(1+\sqrt{\Delta_T^\star})$ without knowing $\Delta_T^\star$, up to domain and gradient-scale factors. The bound is minimax optimal, including its domain dependence, on $\ell_p$ balls for every $1\le p\le\infty$. No smoothness or condition bounding gradient norms by loss values is assumed, and neither loss values nor the time horizon are needed. Algorithmically, we extend the metric-update revocation of Gupta et al. (2026), originally developed for online inverse optimization, to general OCO using the follow-the-regularized-leader framework and a mixture of multiple base learners. We also apply our algorithm to contextual search and online inverse optimization. In $d$-dimensional contextual search with comparison flips of unknown bias $\kappa>0$, we obtain minimax expected cumulative absolute error $\Theta(\sqrt{d}/\kappa^2)$ under Euclidean normalization using polynomially many arithmetic operations, resolving an open question of Liu et al. (2021). For online inverse optimization with the Euclidean unit-ball utility domain, our algorithm bounds the regret relative to observed actions by $O(\sqrt{d}+d^{1/4}\sqrt{D_T})$, where $D_T$ is their cumulative suboptimality; this matches the regret rate of Gupta et al. (2026) when $D_T=0$. Our guarantee extends to other utility domains; for example, on the probability simplex, the bound improves to $O(\log(d+1)+\sqrt{D_T\log(d+1)})$.

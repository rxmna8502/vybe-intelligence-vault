# StableJudge: A Lightweight Adversarial Defense Framework for LLM-as-a-Judge

## Summary

arXiv:2610.16814v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used as judges to assign scalar scores that drive downstream decision-making, including tasks such as paper review and recommendation. However, LLM-as-a-Judge pipelines are vulnerable to score-hijacking prompt injection embedded in evaluated content, which corrupts scoring signals and distorts induced rankings. Existing defenses often enforce robustness by modifying the judge model itself, making safety dependent on a specific model's scale and training and requiring repeated effort when the judge is updated or replaced. We propose StableJudge, a lightweight defense framework that externalizes prompt-injection mitigation as a reusable pre-scoring capability. StableJudge centers on a compact 0.13B Sentry that localizes injected spans and performs targeted sanitization prior to evaluation, and is trained using adversarially evolving attacks to improve robustness beyond a fixed prompt set. To stabilize measurement, StableJudge derives deterministic, fine-grained scalar scores from the judge's output distribution. Across held-out scientific abstracts and out-of-distribution attack variants, StableJudge improves score recovery under attack while largely preserving general-purpose performance, demonstrating that robust LLM-based judging can be achieved by decoupling safety from judge scale via reusable defense capabilities. Code and data will be released.

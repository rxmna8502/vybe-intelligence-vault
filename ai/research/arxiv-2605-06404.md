# FRInGe: Distribution-Space Integrated Gradients with Fisher--Rao Geometry

## Summary

arXiv:2605.06404v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Although gradient-based attribution methods are model-faithful and scalable, a prominent variant, Integrated Gradients (IG), suffers from high sensitivity to design choices, particularly in baseline selection, path formulation, and discretization, as well as gradient saturation. To address these limitations, we propose Fisher--Rao Integrated Gradients (FRInGe), which defines both the reference and integration paths in the predictive distribution space. FRInGe replaces input baselines with a maximum-entropy predictive reference and follows a Fisher--Rao geodesic on the probability simplex. The corresponding input-space trajectory is realized through the pullback Fisher metric and stabilized by Kullback--Leibler (KL) and Euclidean trust regions; attributions are obtained by integrating input gradients along this trajectory. We further derive FRInGe-B, a variant that binarizes the predictive probability distribution, removing the computationally expensive Fisher products of FRInGe. Across six ImageNet architectures, FRInGe most clearly improves Magnitude Aligned Scoring (MAS), while remaining competitive on perturbation area-under-the-curve (AUC) metrics and infidelity.

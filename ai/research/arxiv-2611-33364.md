# Discovering Explicit Magnetic Core-Loss Equations via Symbolic Sparse Identification

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33364v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Compact core loss equations are essential tools in magnetic component design, but existing approaches trade accuracy against interpretability. Empirical models such as the Steinmetz Equation (SE) yield compact expressions of limited accuracy, while machine learning methods achieve high accuracy as black-box predictors. This paper bridges the gap with a Symbolic Sparse Identification (SSI) framework, which casts core loss modeling as a data-driven symbolic regression problem and recovers unknown equations directly from measured data, without presuming a functional form. First, a comprehensively expanded library of symbolic candidate terms far richer than the SE is constructed. Second, the governing terms are isolated from this library by sparse regression. Third, the coefficients of the retained terms are refined with an AdamW optimizer to maximize accuracy and form an analytical equation. Experiments on Fair-Rite 78, 79, and 95 yield test mean absolute percentage errors of 1.89%, 1.01%, and 1.56%, respectively, with only 10, 10, and 7 active terms. Comparisons with empirical, numerical, and symbolic models quantify the resulting balance of accuracy and compactness. SSI thus provides an explicit and physically guided approach for high-frequency magnetic characterization and design.

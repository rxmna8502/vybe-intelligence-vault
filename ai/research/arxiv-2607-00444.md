# Spatiotemporal and Multi-Robot Motion Planning on Graphs of Space-Time Convex Sets

## Summary

arXiv:2607.00444v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Decoupled multi-robot motion planning avoids the high-dimensional joint configuration space but requires repeated single-robot planning among moving obstacles, where route choice and timing are coupled. We develop a framework based on graphs of space-time convex sets (ST-GCSs) for continuous spatiotemporal planning and multi-robot coordination. An ST-GCS represents collision-free space-time by convex sets, allowing the sequence of regions and the geometry and timing of piecewise-linear trajectories to be optimized jointly under velocity limits. To incorporate moving obstacles and previously planned robots, we develop Exact Convex Decomposition (ECD), which removes their swept occupancies while maintaining a convex decomposition for subsequent planning queries. Building on path-based GCS search, we develop a best-first solver tailored to the ST-GCS motion model, exploiting temporal reachability for dominance pruning and using admissible heuristics with separate or joint search-key evaluation. Precomputed heuristic bounds can also be reused across reservation-updated graphs. We integrate ST-GCS planning and ECD into prioritized multi-robot planning and introduce windowed coordination with dynamic window adjustment to limit reservation-induced graph growth. Experiments demonstrate improved scalability and competitive solution quality against optimization-based and sampling-based planners on challenging spatiotemporal and multi-robot problems. Large-scale simulations coordinate up to $100$ robots within a minute, while real-robot demonstrations showcase the practical applicability of the framework.

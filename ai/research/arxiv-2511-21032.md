# ELBO$_{\mathrm{TDS}}$: Temporal Distribution Shift Generalization for Industry-Scale Recommender Systems

## Summary

arXiv:2511.21032v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Temporal distribution shift (TDS) degrades the generalization of recommender systems: models trained on past interactions face systematically different user behavior in future time windows. While incremental training on recent data is widely used in industrial production, it can over-adapt to transient patterns and exacerbate instability. Existing approaches such as invariant learning and self-supervised learning offer partial solutions but often suffer from unstable temporal generalization, representation collapse, or inefficient data utilization. To address these limitations, we propose ELBO$_{\mathrm{TDS}}$, a probabilistic framework for temporally robust recommendation. First, we identify key shifting factors in real-world production data and design a simple yet effective data augmentation strategy that simulates realistic time-varying factors and expands the training support. Second, we derive a self-supervised variational objective, ELBO$_{\mathrm{TDS}}$, to harness the benefits of this extended distribution while preventing representation collapse. Extensive experiments on both a public benchmark and a large-scale industrial dataset demonstrate that ELBO$_{\mathrm{TDS}}$ consistently improves next-period ranking performance over strong baselines. It achieves a 2.33\% uplift in GMV per user in online experiments and has been successfully deployed in production. Code is available at https://github.com/FuCongResearchSquad/ELBO4TDS

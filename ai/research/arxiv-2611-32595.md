# Degree-bounded Satisfiability of Local MPNNs with Sum Aggregation is NEXPTIME-hard

## Summary

arXiv:2611.32595v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We prove that the following problem is NEXPTIME-hard: given a message passing neural network (MPNN) $M$ with local sum aggregation and truncated ReLU activations, and a degree bound $d$ encoded in unary, decide whether there is a pointed graph $(G,v)$ with rational node labels and degree at most $d$ that is accepted by $M$. Hardness already holds for the fixed degree bound $d=3$.

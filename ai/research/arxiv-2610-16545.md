# Heuristics are Not Enough: Calibrated and Corrected Caching for Video Diffusion Acceleration

## Summary

arXiv:2610.16545v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Feature caching has become a mainstream training-free strategy for accelerating video diffusion models by reusing intermediate outputs across denoising steps. However, existing adaptive methods rely on heuristic difference measures misaligned with actual generation quality, employ a single global threshold that ignores heterogeneous phase dynamics, and never validate their budget estimates against true approximation error, allowing systematic bias to propagate uncorrected. We propose \textbf{AlignCache}, a closed-loop caching framework that directly aligns scheduling decisions with their true influence on the final generated video through three tightly coupled components: (1) phase-adaptive budget routing that partitions the denoising trajectory into structurally distinct phases, each governed by an independent threshold and a staleness-aware guard; (2) closed-loop threshold calibration that exploits counterfactual error measurement at the classifier-free guided output level to adjust decision boundaries online via an EMA feedback loop; and (3) momentum-augmented draft synthesis combining direction-magnitude gated residual extrapolation with anchor-guided unconditional correction. By transforming scheduling from a static risk accumulator into a feedback-driven, self-correcting controller, AlignCache consistently achieves superior acceleration-quality trade-offs across state-of-the-art video generation models. Our code is available at https://github.com/jiangjyjy/AlignCache.

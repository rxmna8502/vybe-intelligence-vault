# Relative Discharge Stage Classification from Battery Sensor Measurements in Laboratory Discharge Tests

## Summary

arXiv:2609.27986v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Relative discharge stage (RDS) is an ordinal target obtained by dividing the realized remaining discharge time by the total duration of the same discharge episode and assigning the ratio to a bin. We investigate whether these bins can be predicted from measurements available up to the present sample. The framework combines an electrothermal equivalent-circuit model and adaptive extended Kalman filter to estimate state of charge (SOC), then classifies discharge stage using the estimated SOC, current, terminal voltage, and surface temperature. With the evaluated two-layer convolutional network, the final-time prediction directly uses the three most recent observations. Earlier measurements can influence the output through the recursive SOC estimate. On complete discharge tests, the five-stage classifier achieved accuracies of 95.38% and 81.23% for FTP-75 and PDMHC and 76.41% and 73.87% for LCO_5 and LCO_6, respectively. These results characterize classification within the laboratory protocols. Incremental value over SOC alone, reference-based SOC accuracy, and utility in vehicle operation remain to be established.

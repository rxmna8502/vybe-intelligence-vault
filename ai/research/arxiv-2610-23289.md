# Data-Efficient Classifier Pretraining Through Diffusion Models

## Summary

arXiv:2610.23289v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Machine learning with limited data is susceptible to overfitting, especially with modern deep neural networks. Prior to the introduction of AlexNet, reconstruction-based pretraining with auto-encoder and denoising auto-encoder were found to be beneficial, partially due to the limited size of the training sets at the time. Motivated by these traditional approaches, we propose DiffPT, a simple diffusion modeling approach to pretrain a given classifier backbone for limited domain-specific training data. We experiment on time-series data, standard natural-image benchmarks, and medical imaging datasets, and contrast the diffusion approach to a multitude of modern and traditional un/self-supervised methods. While contrastive pretraining performs better on CIFAR-10 and Tiny ImageNet in low-data regimes, DiffPT is particularly effective on medical imaging and time-series tasks, where it outperforms self-supervised baselines across a range of low-data regimes. Our results suggest that DiffPT is a simple and effective approach for data-efficient classifier pretraining on different modalities.

# Piano2Pop: Expanding Piano Demos into Pop Productions Without Aligned Supervision

## Summary

arXiv:2610.31510v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We introduce Piano2Pop, a music-expansion task that transforms piano MIDI into instrumental pop productions. Enabled by recent advances in music generation and polyphonic music transcription, our pseudo-supervised approach conditions a pretrained flow-matching model on piano rolls without aligned piano-pop supervision. We train on synthetic piano-instrumental pairs derived from pop recordings, comparing pair-free transcription, including function-selective (FST) and register-density (RDA) augmentations, with a pair-supervised piano-cover generator as pseudo-demo sources. On 476 testing real-world piano-cover/pop pairs, the pair-supervised approach achieves the best Frechet Audio Distance, Accompaniment Prompt Adherence, and spectral shape on real-piano inputs, while FST mitigates piano-heavy outputs in pair-free training and remains a promising alternative. All fine-tuned systems significantly outperform the zero-shot baselines, which suggests a role for task-specific modeling.

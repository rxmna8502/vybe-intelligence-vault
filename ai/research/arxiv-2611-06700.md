# PINR-ESI: Physics-Informed Self-Supervised Implicit Neural Representation Framework for EEG Extended-Source Imaging

## Summary

arXiv:2611.06700v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: EEG extended-source imaging aims to localize active cortical regions and characterize their extent, yet supervised inverse networks typically depend on synthetic EEG-source pairs and may be sensitive to synthetic-to-real mismatch. We propose a physics-informed self-supervised implicit neural representation (INR) framework for spatial source reconstruction without paired source labels or simulation-based supervised pretraining. The lead-field matrix encodes the source-to-sensor volume-conduction model and constrains the INR through agreement between predicted and measured EEG. Cortical current density is represented as a continuous coordinate-based neural field. Forward EEG prediction samples the field at cortical vertices and applies local smoothing before lead-field mapping. A classical inverse estimate provides warm initialization and observation-specific spatial guidance through prior-guided support regularization. Simulations across varying SNRs, source extents, and source counts showed low spatial dispersion and good localization performance. Quantitative evaluation on clinical EEG recordings from epilepsy patients and qualitative analysis of scalp EEG during intracranial stimulation supported feasibility for real-world source imaging. These findings suggest the potential of physics-informed self-supervised INR for extended-source imaging.

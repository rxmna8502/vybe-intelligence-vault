# Universal Semantic Enhancement for Weakly Supervised Anomaly Detection

## Summary

arXiv:2610.23765v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Weakly supervised anomaly detection on tabular data is an essential task in many label-scarce applications, since tabular data is widely encountered and it is commonly observed in practice that only a limited number of labeled anomalies are available alongside abundant unlabeled samples. Most existing methods try to learn features that capture the inherent distribution patterns in the target domain. However, such features often struggle to provide a complete description of possible abnormal characteristics and are highly susceptible to overfitting the extremely scarce labels. To address this challenge, we propose a weakly supervised anomaly detection method based on universal semantic enhancement for tabular data, which fuses the universal knowledge from a pre-trained tabular foundation model with the domain-specific knowledge learned on the target dataset. The two types of knowledge are naturally complementary: universal semantics describe the common patterns of tabular data across domains, while the domain-specific features capture the inherent distributions of normal and anomalous behaviors in the target dataset. A prototype-conditioned modulation mechanism is then designed to effectively integrate the two types of features, allowing them to reinforce each other for improved anomaly detection. Extensive experiments on public datasets show that the proposed method outperforms competitive methods when only eight labeled anomalies are available in the training procedure.

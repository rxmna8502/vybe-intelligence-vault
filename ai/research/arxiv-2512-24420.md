# Virasoro Symmetry in Neural Network Field Theories

## Summary

arXiv:2512.24420v4 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We construct the Virasoro algebra from a neural free-field architecture. A logarithmic momentum law and a smooth spectral window give the weight-one current kernel, whose radial-ordering discontinuity fixes the oscillator pairing. The limiting neural current correlations agree with the Fock vacuum correlations. Their Sugawara stress tensor gives the Virasoro algebra with central charge one, and charged vertex insertions identify its highest-weight sectors. Bounds on regulator and feature errors control finite-mode approximations to this representation. At finite width, an explicit adjoint-compatible projection defines an auxiliary Fock readout when its Hermitian mixed block is positive. Cauchy features extend the construction to fermionic and supersymmetric sectors, including a Clifford zero mode in the Ramond representation, while first-order stress tensors give fermionic and bosonic ghost systems. Shared reflected features impose scalar boundary conditions in every realization and yield conformal and superconformal gluing in the limiting fields. Numerical comparisons support the current pairing and field correlations; their detailed analysis is collected in the appendices.

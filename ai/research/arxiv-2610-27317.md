# Machine-learned spectral PDE solvers for fluids and plasmas

## Summary

arXiv:2610.27317v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Sparse regression methods for discovering governing partial differential equations (PDEs) from data are sensitive to observation noise and are not constrained to reproduce the data under forward integration. We address both limitations by performing weak-form discovery in the spatial discretization in which the PDE is solved, learned by differentiating through multistep rollouts anchored at the observed states. The new method, Weak-form Anchored Rollout for PDEs (WARP), produces a machine-learned PDE solver. In this work, we focus on systems amenable to a spectral Galerkin solver. Across eight benchmark fluid and plasma systems, including high-dimensional turbulent simulations, WARP produces numerically stable rollouts from new random initial conditions and substantially improves coefficient recovery, rollout accuracy, and statistical measures compared with a single-step weak-form SINDy baseline.

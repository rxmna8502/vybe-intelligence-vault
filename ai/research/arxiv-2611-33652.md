# Self-supervised Clipped-objective Reinforcement Leaning Framework for Risk-aware Portfolio Management

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33652v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Portfolio management involves balancing diverse financial assets, a critical yet challenging task in quantitative trading. Although deep reinforcement learning has emerged as an active research domain for solving complex sequential decision-making problems over the past decade, deploying reinforcement learning frameworks in non-stationary financial markets raises formidable difficulties. Most existing reinforcement learning-based portfolio management models fail to account for tick-level intraday market dynamics (e.g., order flow imbalances and liquidity fluctuations), which reflect rapid capital movements. Simultaneously, these models suffer from unstable learning processes due to reinforcement learning's inherent high variance. This paper presents a Self-supervised Clipped-objective Portfolio management framework (SCP), which addresses these limitations through a two-phase architecture. Market feature integration phase (application contribution) integrates micro-level market microstructure patterns with macro-level financial indicators to enhance sensitivity to intraday asset trend dynamics. Policy optimization Phase (AI contribution) employs intermediate asset state representations to derive optimal portfolio allocations via a self-supervised clipped-objective policy-learner, ensuring both effectiveness and computational efficiency. Extensive evaluations on three major index datasets from China's A-share market demonstrate that SCP's superior performance in balancing risk-return trade-offs compared to state-of-the-art baselines. Exploratory and ablation studies further validate the contributions of SCP's key components.

# BEAVER: An Efficient Deterministic LLM Verifier

## Summary

arXiv:2512.05439v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: As large language models (LLMs) transition from research prototypes to production systems, practitioners need reliable ways to verify model outputs before they are deployed. While sampling-based estimates provide an ad-hoc intuition of model behavior, they offer no sound guarantees. We present BEAVER, the first practical framework for computing deterministic, sound bounds on the probability that an LLM's response to a prompt satisfies a property. Given a prompt and an oracle, BEAVER systematically explores the model's output distribution using a *token trie* and a *frontier*, maintaining provably sound bounds at every iteration. We formalize the LLM verification problem, prove that BEAVER's bounds are sound and that its selection strategy Max-$\mu$ is optimal for verification upto any tolerance. On 3 important safety tasks across 5 popular LLMs, BEAVER certifies up to $7.6\times$ more prompts as risky than a sampling-based verifier while using fewer forward passes, and its per-prompt bounds extend to statistical certificates over distributions of prompts.

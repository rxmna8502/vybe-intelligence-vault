# AdapTS: Lightweight Teacher-Student Approach for Multi-Class and Continual Visual Anomaly Detection

## Summary

arXiv:2603.17530v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Visual Anomaly Detection (VAD) is crucial for industrial inspection. However, most methods are designed for single-category models, and Teacher-Student (TS) approaches, despite their efficiency, have not been designed for the Continual scenario, where they need a replay buffer. To bridge this gap, we propose AdapTS, a unified TS framework for multi-class and continual settings, optimized for resource-constrained deployment. A single shared frozen backbone acts as teacher and student, and lightweight trainable adapters, selected at inference by a prototype-based task identification (99% accuracy), store the knowledge of each category without keeping past data. Training is enhanced via a segmentation-guided objective on synthetic Perlin noise. On MVTec AD, AdapTS-M improves I-ROC by 11 points over STFPM and needs 4.3 times less memory than Dinomaly, which is 4 points ahead. In the Continual scenario, AdapTS-M reaches the best pixel-level AP with only 0.1% forgetting and no replay buffer. AdapTS-S needs only 23 MB, and with a MobileNetV2 backbone AdapTS needs 6-7 MB while outperforming the edge-oriented STFPM on all metrics. AdapTS is thus a viable solution for resource-constrained deployment.

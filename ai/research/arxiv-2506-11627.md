# Evaluating and Mitigating Skin Color Bias in Skin Lesion Classificatio

## Summary

arXiv:2506.11627v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Skin lesion classifiers may overlook performance disparity because of subtle skin tone variations by treating skin tone as a categorical attribute. This study proposes a method that represents skin tone as a continuous attribute and assigns individual weights to each sample based on its nonlinear relationship with classification performance. Evaluations on two dermatological datasets showed that fairness metrics were sensitive to skin-color categorization. The proposed method also demonstrated more stable fairness improvements than conventional categorical reweighting while generally preserving classification performance.

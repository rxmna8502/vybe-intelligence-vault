# Large Decision Models: Repurposing Large Language Models as Single-Pass Decision Makers

## Summary

arXiv:2610.15501v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Large language models make decisions through parallel prefill and sequential decoding. Although effective, this approach incurs decoding costs and does not explicitly expose decision probabilities. Existing task-specific classifiers require per-task training and offer limited generalization to unseen decision schemas. We propose Large Decision Models (LDMs), repurposing language models to return explicit probabilities over labels specified at run time using one prefill forward pass. We first adapt models to this interface through Diverse Decision Supervision (DDS), covering varied domains, rules and labels. To further narrow the performance gap to a reasoning model, Self-Posed Reasoning Distillation (SPRD) adds tasks generated by a frozen teacher, filters them for answer consistency, and trains on label distributions obtained from repeated teacher reasoning. We evaluate on DecisionVista, pairing new-schema tests with held-out human-labelled sources. Across three base models, the recipe improves accuracy over a step-matched baseline by 1.8 and 1.5 percentage points, respectively, achieving a 182-fold speedup in latency over reasoning rollout on Gemma-4-31B. Reasoning remains stronger on the new-schema test, motivating a confidence router for uncertain decisions. On development sets, routing recovers a median 92% of reasoning's accuracy advantage while generating 27-58% of its tokens. Code is available at https://github.com/JianhuiWei7/LDM.

# FlexCast: Flexible Context Learning for Time Series Foundation Models

## Summary

arXiv:2611.17862v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Time series foundation models (TSFMs) based on autoregressive (AR) next-token prediction entangle two factors: variable-context conditioning and prediction-unit factorization with autoregressive rollout. Strong AR performance therefore does not reveal whether generalization stems from the flexible training distribution, the local prediction target, the left-to-right inference process, or their combination. We propose FlexCast, a flexible context-target learning framework that disentangles these factors. FlexCast reformulates forecasting as variable-length masked prediction over time series patches: during pretraining, it dynamically samples visible context and masked target patches, exposing the model to diverse context-target configurations without sequential decoding. At inference time, the same pretrained model supports one-step forecasting, confidence-based iterative refinement, and next-token-style generation through a unified mask-and-predict interface, enabling controlled comparison of inference schedules under identical architecture, data, and objective. Evaluated on BLAST and multiple zero-shot benchmarks, FlexCast achieves accuracy competitive with or better than strong TSFM baselines while substantially reducing inference latency. Ablations confirm that context diversity and contiguous target masking drive performance, while left-to-right decoding does not yield consistent gains once flexible context-target relationships have been learned, suggesting that the key benefit of AR-style TSFMs lies in flexible training rather than sequential inference. The code is available at https://github.com/BenchCouncil/FlexCast.

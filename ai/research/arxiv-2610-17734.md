# Explore with Memory, Learn without It: In-Context Exploration and Self-Distillation for LLM Reasoning

## Summary

arXiv:2610.17734v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Exploration bounds learning efficiency in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). Current methods explore mostly through independent sampling, without fully using the information gathered along the way, such as known wrong answers and environment feedback. We propose LEAF, which injects a dynamic, problem-specific memory into the context before every rollout. The memory contains the most recent response, its reward, and other environment feedback. It is updated after every verifier call. We decouple policy updates from rollouts and update the policy through self-distillation. On four benchmarks and two open models, LEAF accelerates exploration by about 4x compared with a random sampling baseline. On unseen test sets, it improves accuracy over the strongest baseline by 1.59 and 1.83 percentage points on the two models, while using less compute than any RL baseline. These results highlight the potential of LEAF in improving both exploration and learning efficiency. Code is available at: https://github.com/on1262/LEAF

# Sampling the XY model with an attention-based neural network

## Summary

arXiv:2610.23783v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We present a transformer-based variational autoregressive network for sampling lattice systems with continuous degrees of freedom and apply it to the two-dimensional XY model near the Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless transition. The architecture is combined with physics-informed ingredients: locality-informed attention and approximate probabilities. The resulting sampler significantly outperforms existing neural samplers. We train the model on lattices up to $96\times96$, verify with a mode-dropping estimator that no parts of the configuration space are missed, and reproduce the energy, magnetization and helicity modulus obtained with the traditional Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithm.

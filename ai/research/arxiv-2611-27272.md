# Error-Corrected Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Optimal Control of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles under Uncertainty

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27272v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We solve open-loop optimal control problems under parametric uncertainty using a physics-informed DeepONet that returns trajectories for all sampled scenarios in one evaluation. Trained only on the residual at generic controls, the operator is reused across targets and horizons. Because a gradient-based optimizer exploits surrogate errors, we correct the prediction by one Newton step on the dynamics, computed for all scenarios and times by one parallel scan. The corrected state has a second-order error bounded by the residual alone, and the corrected cost is honest for every control up to the sampling error. On a fixed-wing UAV benchmark, the corrected operator matches the accuracy of a batched Adam solver and an Ipopt solver at equal sample size and is 8 times faster than the Adam solver on the same GPU at a few hundred scenarios and 15 times faster than Ipopt on a CPU.

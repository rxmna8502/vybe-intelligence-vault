# The Optimal Sample Complexity of Agnostic PAC Learning for Partial Concept Classes

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27946v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Let $\mathcal H\subseteq\{-1,+1,\star\}^{\mathcal X}$ be a partial concept class of finite VC dimension $d$. We construct a deterministic agnostic PAC learner that, for every $\varepsilon,\delta\in(0,1)$, uses $O((d+\log(1/\delta))/\varepsilon^2)$ i.i.d. examples from an arbitrary distribution $\mathcal D$ over $\mathcal X\times\{-1,+1\}$ and returns a total concept $\hat h:\mathcal X\to\{-1,+1\}$ satisfying $\text{er}_{\mathcal D}(\hat h)\leq \min_{h\in\mathcal H}\text{er}_{\mathcal D}(h)+\varepsilon$ with probability at least $1-\delta$, where $\text{er}_{\mathcal D}(h)=\mathbb P_{(x,y)\sim\mathcal D}[h(x)\neq y]$. This establishes the optimal sample complexity, matching the lower bound of Alon et al. (FOCS 2021).

# DISRL: Dual-Scale Incremental Semi-Supervised Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Community Tracking

## Summary

arXiv:2610.29315v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Dynamic local community detection tracks the evolving community of designated seed nodes across graph snapshots. However, it faces two critical challenges: temporal supervision uncertainty, where sparse and outdated labels fail to provide seed-specific guidance under topology changes, and unbounded error cascades, where sequential policy transfer accumulates exploration errors over long horizons. To address these challenges, we propose DISRL, a Dual-scale Incremental Semi-supervised Reinforcement Learning framework. DISRL introduces a supervision construction module that dynamically generates seed-specific guidance from sparse known communities, and a dual-scale community exploration module that stabilizes policy evolution by decoupling short-term adaptation within temporal windows from long-term parameter consolidation across windows. Furthermore, we construct a dynamic graph benchmark with diverse community evolution scenarios. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DISRL consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines under various dynamic settings.

# FokasNets: Completing missing boundary data in Physics-Informed Neural Networks and Operators via the Global Relation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33549v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We introduce FokasNets, a framework that combines Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and operators (PINOs) with the Global Relation (GR) formulation of the Fokas method (also referred to as the Unified Transform), for mixed boundary problems. The general framework considers lifted representations of the neural network models, where the Dirichlet boundary conditions are imposed by construction and the neural network learns the interior correction. The Global Relation is used as an additional solve during training that provides the missing boundary conditions to the lifted neural network, making training more efficient and accurate in the interior and on the boundary. We develop the construction of FokasNets for elliptic and evolution PDEs (Fokas-PINNs), and the corresponding PDE solution operator problems (Fokas Operator Networks - FONets), using the Global Relation formulations for these PDE classes. Furthermore, we develop the necessary theoretical framework for FokasNets, including error and stability analysis and show that FokasNets are universal approximators. We provide comprehensive numerical experiments, using examples with manufactured solutions and literature benchmarks. Compared to standard PINNs and Physics-Informed DeepONets and their lifted versions, FokasNets achieve significant improvements in solution accuracy in the interior and boundaries, both for single PDEs and particularly for solution operators, where the improvement reaches multiple orders of magnitude and fixed threshold errors are reached faster during training.

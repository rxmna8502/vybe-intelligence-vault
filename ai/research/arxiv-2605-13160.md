# Kernel-based guarantees for nonlinear parametric models in Bayesian optimization

## Summary

arXiv:2605.13160v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Modern Bayesian optimization and adaptive sampling methods increasingly rely on nonlinear parametric models, such as neural networks, yet theoretical guarantees for such models under adaptive data collection remain limited. Existing analyses largely focus on Gaussian processes, kernel machines, linear models, or linearized neural approximations, leaving a gap between theory and the nonlinear models used in practice. We develop a kernel-based framework for analyzing regularized parametric models trained on adaptively collected data. Our approach uses kernels over the parameter space to induce reproducing kernel Hilbert space structures over the corresponding model class, yielding confidence bounds for models trained with a broad class of regularized losses. We show how these bounds can establish convergence guarantees for approximate Thompson sampling and provide a foundation for analyzing broader likelihood-free Bayesian optimization methods. These results provide a unified route to analyzing nonlinear models in Bayesian optimization and related adaptive optimization settings.

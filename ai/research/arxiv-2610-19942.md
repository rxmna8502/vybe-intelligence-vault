# MorphingNet: Symbolic Regression Where the Network Is the Formula

## Summary

arXiv:2610.19942v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We introduce MorphingNet, a network for symbolic regression (SR) whose units change their function continuously. Instead of searching over expressions built from a fixed operator library, MorphingNet trains the form of each function as a parameter, so SR becomes one continuous optimization problem. Its units are either pole-free rational functions or complex exponential--logarithm functions, which move between exponentials, sinusoids, damped oscillations, power laws and logarithms as their parameters change. The network is a readable formula at every step of training, and no operator library is needed to train it; a structure-recovery step afterwards rewrites the trained formula into short elementary expressions. Training is dense linear algebra, so it is GPU-friendly and scales to large data. MorphingNet achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on SRBench, recovers most generating formulas on synthetic benchmarks, and on large data is up to two orders of magnitude faster than the strongest search method. By turning the search for a formula into the training of a network, MorphingNet offers a new paradigm for SR and for scientific discovery from data.

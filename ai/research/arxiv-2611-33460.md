# Fast Muscle-Driven Whole-Body Motion Tracking with a Differentiable World Model

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33460v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Tracking motion capture with a whole-body musculoskeletal model means coordinating hundreds of muscles through contact, and current controllers take hours to days of GPU time to train, so every tuning decision is expensive. SuperMimic trains a tracking policy by backpropagating through the rollouts of a learned world model. We extend it to MS-Human-700 (700 muscles) and MyoFullBody (354 muscles), with MuJoCo's activation dynamics inside the world model. To make its training fast, our recipe takes the speedups developed for the Unitree G1, replaces three of their settings that fail on muscles, and adds four changes of our own: observations without muscle geometry, data collection concurrent with the updates, Beta exploration noise that keeps every excitation in $[0,1]$, and 50 Hz control. On one RTX 5090 it trains MS-Human-700 in 6.3 minutes to the held-out return that SuperMimic's published hyperparameters reach in 3.9 hours, 37$\times$ sooner, and in 20 minutes to lower tracking errors than theirs after 10 hours. It trains MyoFullBody 34$\times$ sooner without retuning. Ablations show what is specific to muscles: a higher policy learning rate stalls half of the runs, and clipped Gaussian noise adds a tonic co-contraction that Beta noise avoids. Finally, refining the policy's actions through the world model at test time lowers the position error by a further 26% with no held-out clip failing, and reaches the same return after 5.1 instead of 6.3 minutes of training. Code, the training recipe and videos are on the project page https://muscle-supermimic.beanpow.top.

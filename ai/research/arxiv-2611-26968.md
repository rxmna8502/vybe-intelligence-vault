# Right Numbers, Wrong Story: When LLMs Narrate Financial Changes Beyond Their Inputs

## Summary

arXiv:2611.26968v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: A financial report drafted by an LLM can state every number correctly but still go beyond its input, adding judgments and causes that no one supplied. We call these additions unsupported framing and find them far more often in LLM drafts than in reports written by proInput facts Revenue +3% Higher prices 10-K filings Revenue increased 3% on higher prices. 10-K writer Revenue surged 3% on higher prices, due to strong demand. LLM Fits the size? Surged: Unusual for 3% In the input? In the input? Strong demand: Not in input Higher prices: Kept fessionals, and attempts to fix them by prompting with how each word is used instead make such judgments more frequent. We therefore propose FrameCheck, a plug-in that sits beFrameCheck Revenue increased 3% on higher prices. No extra model call Judgment word Unsupported cause Supported cause tween the LLM and human review to keep each draft within its input. Specifically, FrameCheck 1) guides the draft with professional examples that report a change of similar magnitude, 2) flags judgments that professionals would not use and causes the input does not give, and 3) removes them by rule, without a model call. In experiments on 17 open LLMs and 3M drafts, FrameCheck removes almost all unsupported framing from drafts and brings the judgment rate close to that of the writers of 10-K filings, the annual reports that US firms file with regulators. Code is available at github.com/seunghan96/framecheck.

# Yet Another Concept Drift Benchmark? A Principled Framework for Evaluating Drift Detectors

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33424v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Concept drift detection is a fundamental component of data stream learning, yet its empirical evaluation remains based on loosely defined protocols, ambiguous alarm semantics, and inconsistent metrics. We propose a principled benchmark grounded in an explicit formulation of drift observability and alarm semantics. Our framework introduces finite detection windows and repeated alarms, enabling a clear separation between detection accuracy, timeliness, and stability, and employs a reproducible observer to provide a controlled and interpretable performance signal. Building on this formulation, we define normalized evaluation metrics and a composite score addressing limitations of existing measures. We validate the framework through a large-scale stress-testing evaluation of 10 detectors across 9 learner configurations and 16 datasets.

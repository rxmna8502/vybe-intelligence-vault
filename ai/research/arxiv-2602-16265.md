# On sparsity, extremal structure, and monotonicity properties of Wasserstein and Gromov-Wasserstein optimal transport plans

## Summary

arXiv:2602.16265v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: This note gives a self-contained overview of some key properties of the Gromov-Wasserstein (GW) distance, compared with the standard linear optimal transport (OT) framework. More specifically, I explore the following questions: are GW optimal transport plans sparse? under what conditions does there exist an optimal plan supported on a permutation? do optimal plans satisfy a form of cyclical monotonicity? In particular, I present the conditionally negative/positive definite (CND/CPD) properties and show that, when they hold, there exist GW optimal plans that are sparse and supported on a permutation. In the last section, I also give examples where no permutation is optimal when the CND or CPD assumption is dropped.

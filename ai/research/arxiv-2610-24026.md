# Dynamical Kernel Attention: Estimating the Present from a Record of the Past

## Summary

arXiv:2610.24026v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Self-attention encodes where a past token lies, but not how its uncertainty grows with time: evidence accumulates without bound as the context grows, and nothing indicates which tokens are safe to discard. We introduce Dynamical Kernel Attention (DKA), which derives attention from robust estimation of a latent state governed by a stochastic differential equation. Under this model, each head draws bounded evidence from any context. This lets each head abstain when relevant evidence is weak, and gives each head a memory window outside which discarding tokens changes its output by a provably bounded amount. On language modeling, DKA outperforms RoPE within the training length and ALiBi far beyond it, and matches sliding-window attention with a substantially smaller key-value cache.

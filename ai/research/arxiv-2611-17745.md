# The Polyak-{\L}ojasiewicz Paper: A 10-Year Retrospective

## Summary

arXiv:2611.17745v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: In 2016, we wrote a paper introducing the phrase "Polyak-Lojasiewicz (PL) condition" for an old idea, unsure of how readers would react. The paper ultimately had a larger impact than we expected, and we are surprised and honoured to receive the 2026 PKDD ECML Test of Time Award. With 10 years of hindsight, this note offers an informal and candid retrospective on the PL condition. We take a look back at the origins of the paper, give an AI-assisted refined version of the main theorem, review criticisms of the work, and discuss a variety of related ideas and extensions.

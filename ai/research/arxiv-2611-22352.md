# Fair Bubble Sort: Provably Optimal Fair Ranking with Continuous Sensitive Attributes

## Summary

arXiv:2611.22352v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Most fair-ranking methods assume categorical protected attributes, requiring continuous attributes such as age or income to be discretized into groups. This introduces arbitrary thresholds, loses fine-grained information, and ignores the natural ordering of the attribute. In this paper, we study the problem of fair ranking with continuous protected attributes without relying on thresholding. We introduce Continuous Pairwise Parity (CPP), a fairness notion based on Kendall correlation between ranking positions and the protected attribute, and seek rankings that satisfy a prescribed CPP constraint while minimizing their Kemeny distance from an initial score-based ranking. To solve this problem, we propose Fair Bubble Sort (FBS), an efficient post-processing algorithm based on targeted adjacent swaps. We prove that FBS is exactly optimal for the unweighted Kemeny distance and provide approximation guarantees for the score-weighted setting. FBS is output-sensitive, with running time determined by the number of swaps required to meet the fairness constraint. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets show that FBS is scalable and consistently outperforms discretization-based baselines, achieving a superior fairness-accuracy Pareto front.

# SoLiD26: A First Principles Solid-Liquid Interface Dataset for Machine-learned Interatomic Potentials

## Summary

arXiv:2609.28013v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) enable simulations of larger atomistic systems over longer timescales than traditional ab initio methods but require large, diverse training data. Existing datasets lack comprehensive sampling of solid-liquid interfaces, which exhibit complex phenomena such as solvent layering and surface reconstruction critical for energy and catalytic materials. Here, we present SoLiD26, a curated solid-liquid interface dataset of 15.4 million first-principles structures with up to 576 atoms and 15 chemical elements for training and evaluating MLIPs. The structures were compiled from density functional theory (DFT) calculations, with most configurations originating from ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations. SoLiD26 includes aqueous coinage metal interfaces, electrode-electrolyte systems, and selected bulk reference structures, calculated with VASP using the PBE functional and D3 dispersion corrections. The application of SoLiD26 for training and evaluating MLIPs is demonstrated with a suite of MACE models on a simple training, validation and test split. This dataset provides a valuable resource for advancing MLIP development targeted at modelling solid-liquid interfaces in electrochemical and catalytic systems.

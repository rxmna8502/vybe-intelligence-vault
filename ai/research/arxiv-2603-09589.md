# Memorization capacity of deep ReLU neural networks characterized by width and depth

## Summary

arXiv:2603.09589v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: This paper studies the memorization capacity of deep neural networks with ReLU activation. Specifically, we investigate the minimal size of such networks to memorize any $N$ data points in the unit ball with pairwise separation distance $\delta$ and labels from a finite set of size $C$. Most prior studies characterize the memorization capacity by the number of parameters or neurons. We extend these results by constructing ReLU networks with adjustable width $W$ and depth $L$ satisfying $W^2L^2\log W=\mathcal{O}(N(\log(\delta^{-1})+\log C))$ that can memorize any such dataset. We also establish width-depth lower bounds that depend explicitly on $\delta$ and $C$ for networks capable of memorizing all such datasets. When $\log(\delta^{-1})\asymp\log N$, our construction achieves $W^2L^2\asymp N$ for suitable choices of width and depth. This order is optimal if $\log C\asymp\log N$, and optimal up to logarithmic factors if $\log C=o(\log N)$.

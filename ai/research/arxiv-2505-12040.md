# Improving regional weather forecasts with neural interpolation

## Summary

arXiv:2505.12040v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: In this paper we design a neural interpolation operator to improve the boundary data for regional weather models, which is a challenging problem as we are required to map multi-scale dynamics between grid resolutions. In particular, we expose a methodology for approaching the problem through the study of a simplified model, with a view to generalise the results in this work to the dynamical core of regional weather models. Our approach will exploit a combination of techniques from image super-resolution with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and residual networks, in addition to building the flow of atmospheric dynamics into the neural network. We find that building in the flow more than halves the squared error of small networks.

# Risk-Aware Alignment Must Incorporate Reward Uncertainty

## Summary

arXiv:2410.23726v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We consider the problem of policy optimization with noisy rewards, where the policy is constrained to be in the neighborhood of a reference policy.We study the risk of the policy optimization process, defined to be the chance of obtaining a policy worse off than the reference policy. In an idealized multivariate Gaussian model for the noisy reward function, we show that the regularizer achieving optimal risk in a suitably local neighborhood of the reference policy is the quadratic norm weighted by the reward covariance matrix. This result is used to develop a new policy optimization algorithm based on regularization via reward variance estimates. Experiments across synthetic bandit models, large language models and reward models show that this algorithm yields more robust alignment outcomes than existing approaches.

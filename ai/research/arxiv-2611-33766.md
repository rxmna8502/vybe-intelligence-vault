# Don't Waste the Population: Post-Anneal Refinement for Combinatorial Optimization

## Summary

arXiv:2611.33766v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Parallel annealers generate diverse candidate solutions; yet, conventional post-processing typically refines only the best, leaving much of the population's potential untapped. We introduce Iterative Path-Relinking Polish (I-PRP), a deterministic, training-free method that exploits this diversity to discover better solutions without modifying the underlying annealing process. I-PRP selects promising candidates, explores cost-guided paths between locally optimized solutions, and refines intermediate states to uncover better local minima. Newly discovered improvements are iteratively incorporated into the population, enabling further exploration. We theoretically guarantee that I-PRP never degrades solution quality relative to conventional single-candidate polishing on the same population and converges in finitely many iterations. Experiments across challenging combinatorial optimization benchmarks, including MaxCut, MaxClique, and graph coloring, demonstrate substantial improvements in several instances. Paired evaluations confirm non-degradation, while matched-runtime comparisons show that exploiting existing population diversity can outperform additional annealing. These results establish I-PRP as an effective and reliable refinement strategy for parallel annealing.

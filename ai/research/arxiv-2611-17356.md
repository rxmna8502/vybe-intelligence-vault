# Waveform-based pulse timing enhanced by self-supervised neural regression models for digital nuclear instrumentation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.17356v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: With the rapid development of high-sampling-rate digital nuclear devices, their applications in nuclear facilities and related fields become more acceptable, and will possibly become widespread due to the versatility and flexibility of waveform sampling systems. When digitizers reach hundreds of megahertz or gigahertz, their lower limit in time measurement probes into the sub-nanosecond region, expediting their use in a bunch of time-critical scenarios. While conventional algorithms coupled with digital sampling techniques realize fair pulse timing in certain conditions, the potentials are still open to compact and efficient machine learning models. In this paper, we intend to boost the performance of pulse timing with a new paradigm of self-supervised learning on matched waveform pairs. The proposed method combines the mainstream self-supervised loss and the auxiliary weakly-supervised loss on the reference set, jointly improving the timing performance. We apply the method in a gamma/neutron-mixed radiation field, common in nuclear facilities. The simulation and experimental results show that, the proposed method not only outperforms conventional timing algorithms, but also surpasses neural network models based on supervised learning alone. Considering the trained model is lightweight and computationally efficient, it is promising to deploy it on edge devices, like economical microcontrollers with neural processing units.

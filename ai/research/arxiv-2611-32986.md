# CAPER: PartGround and PlanRefine for Reliable Manual-Guided Robotic Assembly Planning

## Summary

arXiv:2611.32986v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Vision-Language-Models (VLMs) used in robot task planning to derive assembly procedures from existing manuals may correctly interpret individual assembly actions yet confuse exact part identities, predict unsupported Part IDs, or produce inconsistent parts across assembly steps. Thus, we propose CAPER (Capacity-Aware Part-grounding and Evidence Refinement), a manual-guided robotic assembly framework that enables VLMs to generate assembly procedures with two modules--PartGround and PlanRefine. PartGround aligns step-level manual evidence with product-specific inventory and part-reference information to identify exact Part IDs and quantities, transforming free-form hardware prediction into evidence-guided assignments represented in a shared Assembly Intermediate Representation (Assembly IR). PlanRefine audits PartGround assignments against finite inventory by removing the lowest-confidence assignments. Uncertain identity cases remain marked for review in the Assembly IR. We evaluate CAPER on the IKEA Assembly in the Wild (IAW) dataset. Experiment results show that CAPER reaches a Finite-inventory Consistency Rate of 83.33% and produces zero observed out-of-inventory Part-ID occurrences. At the plan level, PlanRefine removes all observed inventory-overuse pairs (9 -> 0) and excess units (51 -> 0). Overall, the results indicate that PartGround and PlanRefine improve part reliability, while also enhancing finite-inventory consistency and local prediction accuracy.

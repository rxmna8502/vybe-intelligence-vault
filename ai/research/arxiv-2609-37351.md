# Clause-Factored Bilateral State Grounding and Scoped Passivity for Counterfactual Open-World Deductive Reasoning

## Summary

arXiv:2609.37351v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Open-World Assumption (OWA) deduction requires distinguishing provable facts (True), refutations (False), and unprovable statements (Unknown) across counterfactual edits and unseen domains. Sequence and latent-recurrent transformers rely on distributional shortcuts, whereas chain-of-thought language models incur high autoregressive latency. We decompose OWA deduction into a clause-factored bilateral grounder ($F$) and a deductive deliberation operator ($B$), forming $B \circ F$ in Typed Edge-Factorized Bilateral Port-Hamiltonian Deliberation (TE-BPHD). Trained solely on three-way query labels without parsers or proof-depth annotations, TE-BPHD (37.85M parameters) maps clauses into a 1,440-dimensional Belnap space and propagates support through passivity-balanced implicit Port-Hamiltonian rule diodes. Across 17,036 locked ProofWriter test queries, TE-BPHD reaches 79.17 +/- 5.48% depth-5 counterfactual consistency (+23.83 to +38.67 percentage points over Seq2Seq T5, Flan-T5, and Recurrent-Depth T5) and 94.95 +/- 1.23% out-of-distribution (OOD) accuracy across 5,270 six-domain queries (92.54 +/- 3.38% macro-mean). On a paired 120-query OOD slice, TE-BPHD attains 93.61 +/- 4.59% versus 79.17% for 3-shot Qwen3-1.7B under a 2,048-token reasoning cap (+14.44 percentage points) at 9.44 versus 14,878.4 ms/query batch-16 NVIDIA L4 throughput. Operator interventions show that symmetric single-rule passivity prevents positive two-cycle modes seen in Gain-Matched Unilateral Horn (+50.00 percentage points counterfactual consistency), whereas unit-interval Grounded Product-T-Norm Logic Tensor Networks (LTN) achieve cycle-safe OOD transfer (94.50 +/- 0.87%) without linear solves, and multi-fact ParaRules prose exposes an upstream grounding trade-off (1.33 +/- 1.76% zero-shot vs. 34.11 +/- 5.01% with five-slot optimal-transport joint grounding).

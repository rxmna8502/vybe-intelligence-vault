# ExCal: Optimization-Free Multivariate Time Series Anomaly Detection via Excess Calibration

## Summary

arXiv:2610.35568v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We introduce ExCal, a normal-only multivariate time series anomaly detector that combines raw state retrieval, a signal/derivative subspace, and robust statistics across temporal scales. Each expert map receives a high normality floor from the training prefix. During scoring, positive excess is retained, attenuated for broadly firing maps, and combined with a small rank term. The method fits reference memories and projections without gradient optimization or anomaly labels. Prevalence and ranks depend on the complete scored sequence, making inference batch and transductive rather than online. On 180 series from 17 TSB-AD-M families, ExCal obtains AUC-PR $0.421\pm0.37$ and VUS-PR $0.452\pm0.37$ over three seeds, ranking second among 24 methods. Holm-adjusted paired tests detect no significant difference from AxonAD or StreamVAE, which are the two leading non-pretrained methods on TSB-AD-M, on either primary metric, while finding significant positive differences against the other 21 baselines. A separate single-run probe over three series measures median model-wrapper time of $0.0662$ s. Ablations associate the largest primary-metric changes with raw retrieval and excess-magnitude preservation. The evaluated score is decomposable by expert, but its diagnostic utility and online suitability remain untested.

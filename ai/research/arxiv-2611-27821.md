# Sampling non-abelian lattice gauge theories with diffusion models

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27821v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We present gauge-equivariant diffusion models as samplers for lattice gauge theories (LGTs). Using lattice gauge-equivariant convolutional neural networks (L-CNNs) and the Metropolis-adjusted annealed Langevin algorithm (MAALA), we are able to accurately sample two-dimensional U(2) field configurations and show first promising results for four-dimensional SU(3). Our two-dimensional models extrapolate remarkably well to larger inverse couplings than originally trained on. We confirm this by comparing observables to analytically known values and to hybrid Monte Carlo results.

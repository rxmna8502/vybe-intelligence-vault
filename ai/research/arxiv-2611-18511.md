# On numerical issues of divergences between nearly equal distributions

## Summary

arXiv:2611.18511v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Barron et al. (2026) recently identified numerical issues in the softmax and soft cross-entropy loss. We show that similar issues are present in standard divergence operations between categorical distributions. This includes the forward and reverse Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence and generalized Jensen-Shannon divergence (JSD), as well as Monte Carlo KL estimates and entropy. When the distributions are nearly equal, rounding and cancellation issues affect the forward and gradient computations. These issues can affect settings using divergences in optimization objectives, such as knowledge distillation, reinforcement learning, and proximal methods. We propose numerically stable reformulations and provide example implementations for each setting, validated by property-based tests.

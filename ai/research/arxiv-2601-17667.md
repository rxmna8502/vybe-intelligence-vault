# Entropic Risk-Aware Monte Carlo Tree Search

## Summary

arXiv:2601.17667v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We propose a provably correct Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) algorithm for solving risk-aware Markov decision processes (MDPs) with entropic risk measure (ERM) objectives. We provide a non-asymptotic analysis of our proposed algorithm, showing that the algorithm: (i) is correct in the sense that the empirical ERM obtained at the root node converges to the optimal ERM; and (ii) enjoys polynomial regret concentration. Our algorithm successfully exploits the dynamic programming formulations for solving risk-aware MDPs with ERM objectives introduced by previous works in the context of an upper confidence bound-based tree search algorithm. Finally, we provide a set of illustrative experiments to evaluate our risk-aware MCTS method, showing it successfully trades off between risk-neutral and risk-averse behavior.

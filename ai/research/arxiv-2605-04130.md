# Constrained Extreme Gradient Boosting for Parametric Proper Orthogonal Decomposition Subspace Prediction

## Summary

arXiv:2605.04130v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: High-fidelity simulations such as computational fluid dynamics and finite element analysis are essential for modelling complex engineering systems but remain prohibitively expensive for tasks including parametric studies, optimisation and real-time control. Projection-based reduced-order models (ROMs) alleviate this cost by projecting full-order dynamics onto low-dimensional subspaces. On the other hand, projection-based ROMs can become less robust under parameter variation, and one approach is to adapt the projection basis (that is, the subspace) as the parameters change. We propose constrained extreme gradient boosting (cXGBoost) for predicting the proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) basis at given parameter settings. In particular, given the observed parameter-subspace pairs, we map the subspaces from the Grassmann manifold to a Euclidean space. Then, cXGBoost is trained in this Euclidean space with a constraint that the norm of the predicted vectors is bounded to ensure the injectivity of the mapping between the Euclidean space and the Grassmann manifold. On four parametric problems, a cylinder wake, Kolmogorov flow, the Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equation and a forced beam, cXGBoost has lower mean reconstruction errors than existing approaches, namely the projected Gaussian process and Grassmann interpolation.

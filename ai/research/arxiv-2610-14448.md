# KV4-RL: Unlocking Faster Asynchronous Rollouts for RLVR with INT4 KV Cache Quantization

## Summary

arXiv:2610.14448v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Asynchronous rollout improves the efficiency of reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) by reducing under-utilization during long-tail generation. Sustained concurrency also concentrates context processing into fewer decode steps, increasing the KV workload per step. We characterize this \emph{KV concentration} and find that KV cache quantization delivers substantially greater acceleration than weight-only and weight-activation quantization in our matched-workload comparisons. However, INT4 KV rollout can degrade learning even with token-level truncated importance sampling (TIS). We propose \emph{KV4-RL}, which combines calibration-free INT4 KV quantization with selective INT8 protection of attention sinks and recent context. Protecting a bounded number of positions reduces sampler mismatch while retaining most of the speedup from INT4. Across 400 iterations of Qwen3-8B-Base GRPO training, KV4-RL achieves $1.80\times$ cumulative rollout speedup and $1.27\times$ cumulative full-iteration speedup over BF16 rollout, reaching $1.97\times$ rollout speedup over the final 50 iterations. Under a common BF16 evaluator, its final six-benchmark mean accuracy is within 0.5 percentage points of BF16-rollout training for both Qwen3-4B-Base and Qwen3-8B-Base.

# Adaptable Watermarking: Transforming Image Watermarking Models via Lightweight Adapters

## Summary

arXiv:2611.18363v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We explore the adaptability of neural image watermarking models; the extent to which one watermarking method can be transformed into another through lightweight adaptation. We introduce a framework that enables encoder-decoder watermarking architectures to become modular and upgradable, supporting multiple watermarking styles without re-training full models. Adaptation is performed by attaching a compact parameter-efficient adapter and fine-tuning under a multi-domain residual alignment loss (spatial, gradient, and frequency) while preserving bit accuracy via a secret-loss term. We evaluate this approach across a suite of recent watermarking models, including TrustMark, InvisMark, RoSteALS, HiDDeN, and WAM, demonstrating that adapted encoders closely reproduce target residuals and that adapted decoders recover messages with near-optimal accuracy under standard augmentations and distortions. We show that cross-model adaptation, with applications in interoperable and upgradable watermarking, can be achieved with as few as 1-2M trainable parameters per component (e.g., the 2M decoder adapter used in our multi-decoder inference pipeline).

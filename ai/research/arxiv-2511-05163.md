# Consecutive Preferential Bayesian Optimization

## Summary

arXiv:2511.05163v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Preferential Bayesian optimization allows optimization of objectives that are either expensive or difficult to measure directly, by relying on a minimal number of comparative evaluations done by a human expert. Generating candidate solutions for evaluation is also often expensive, but existing preferential optimization methods do not account for this production cost. We introduce Consecutive Preferential Bayesian Optimization (CPBO) for the consecutive setting, where production cost is reduced by reusing the previous candidate in each comparison. We also account for the perceptual ambiguity of the oracle providing the feedback by incorporating a Just-Noticeable Difference threshold into a probabilistic preference model to capture indifference to small utility differences. We adapt an information-theoretic acquisition strategy to this setting, selecting new configurations that are most informative about the unknown optimum under a preference model accounting for the perceptual ambiguity. We empirically demonstrate a notable increase in accuracy in setups with high production costs or indifference feedback, and demonstrate the practical feasibility of CPBO in a real-world food extrusion task.

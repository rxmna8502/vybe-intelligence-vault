# Integrable Elasticity via Neural Demand Potentials

## Summary

arXiv:2605.22820v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We propose the Integrable Context-Dependent Demand Network (ICDN), a demand-first neural model for multiproduct retail demand. ICDN learns log-demand as a smooth, context-conditioned function of log-prices, allowing elasticities to be derived exactly from the learned demand surface. Across three retail datasets, the model achieves competitive out-of-sample prediction while producing analytically tractable, and economically regularized own- and cross-price responses.

# Mechanistic Interpretability of Cognitive Complexity in LLMs via Linear Probing using Bloom's Taxonomy

## Summary

arXiv:2602.17229v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: The black-box nature of Large Language Models necessitates novel evaluation frameworks that transcend surface-level performance metrics. This study investigates the internal neural representations of the levels of complexity using Bloom's Taxonomy as a hierarchical lens. By analyzing high-dimensional activation vectors from different LLMs, we probe whether different Bloom levels, ranging from basic recall (Remember) to abstract synthesis (Create), are linearly separable within the model's residual streams. Our results demonstrate that linear classifiers achieve high accuracy across all Bloom levels, providing evidence that level information is encoded in a linearly accessible subspace of the model's representations. These findings provide evidence that the model organizes representations according to Bloom categories early in the forward pass, with representations becoming increasingly separable across layers.

# ECPO: Evidence-Coupled Policy Optimization for Verifier-Audited Candidate Ranking

## Summary

arXiv:2605.21993v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Ranking systems used for decision support should expose evidence that can be audited independently of the model's ranking scores. We study a closed-roster setting in which a policy jointly emits a Top-$K$ ranking and candidate-linked, span-grounded evidence bundles. Evidence-Coupled Policy Optimization (ECPO) combines listwise utility, certificate validity, and an evidence-cycle reward that removes answer-bearing fields before deterministic recovery. On MAVEN-ERE and RAMS, ECPO improves verifier-audited traceability, rank-blind recovery, and human comparative support relative to matched controls in the tested provenance settings. The gains persist under held-out evaluators, other 8B/14B backbones, and a HotpotQA answer-ranking interface. Ordinary NDCG and runtime remain explicit trade-offs.

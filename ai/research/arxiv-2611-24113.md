# AG-BFGS: Foundations for Accelerated Geometry Learning

## Summary

arXiv:2611.24113v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We introduce an accelerated geometry-learning framework for BFGS that incorporates momentum into the learning of Hessian inverse approximations. We prove that the resulting accelerated updates satisfy explicit stability conditions and preserve positive definiteness within admissible parameter regions. Numerical experiments demonstrate consistent improvements over the corresponding non-accelerated schemes while maintaining numerical stability. These results provide the first rigorous theoretical foundation for accelerated quasi-Newton learning and bridge the gap between empirical observations and mathematical guarantees for accelerated second-order methods.

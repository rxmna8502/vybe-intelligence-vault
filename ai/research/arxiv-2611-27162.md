# Hierarchical Hybrid-Coordinate Flow Matching for Accurate and Physically Plausible Apo-to-Holo Protein Generation

## Summary

arXiv:2611.27162v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Predicting holo-like protein conformations from apo structures is essential in structure-based drug design. Most existing apo-to-holo models operate in Cartesian space and achieve strong global accuracy. However, high global accuracy does not ensure physically valid local backbone geometry. Internal-coordinate models offer local control and validity, but regressive generation can amplify angular errors, known as the lever-arm effect. We introduce CrypticFlow, a hierarchical flow-matching model that combines these two complementary representations. An ESM-3-conditioned E(3)-equivariant graph network first generates sparse C$\alpha$ anchors in Cartesian space. A conditional midpoint Transformer then recursively fills the gaps in local internal coordinates. During recursive generation, the decoder constrains the distance between each midpoint and any sequence-adjacent parent anchor to the corresponding apo C$\alpha$-C$\alpha$ distance. On the D3PM test split, CrypticFlow achieves the lowest median and mean C$\alpha$ RMSD at published baseline capacities. Across five independent training models, the median and mean direct RMSD are 1.333 $\pm$ 0.056 angstroms and 2.766 $\pm$ 0.041 angstroms, respectively. The quality of local geometry also improves substantially. Our model achieves 99.58 $\pm$ 0.02% adjacent-distance fidelity and 95.87 $\pm$ 0.25% pseudo-angle fidelity, measured within 0.2 angstroms and 20{\deg} of the holo reference. Then, we reconstruct the predicted C$\alpha$-only traces into all-atom structures using the cg2all coarse-to-all-atom backmapping method and evaluate their physical and geometric quality with MolProbity. Our model achieves a MolProbity score of 3.034 $\pm$ 0.054, outperforming the evaluated baselines. These results show that a hierarchical flow-matching model with hybrid coordinates can recover global accuracy while retaining local fidelity.

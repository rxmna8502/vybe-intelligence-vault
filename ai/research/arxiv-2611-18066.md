# Product units in gated recurrent networks enhance prediction of nuclear masses and charge radii

## Summary

arXiv:2611.18066v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: The prediction of nuclear properties using machine learning can complement theoretical models and advance the exploration of poorly known domains of the nuclear chart. We propose a machine learning technique for predicting nuclear masses and charge radii based on gated recurrent units (GRU) that integrates learnable product-unit mappings into the candidate state to model multiplicative dependencies among neighboring nuclei. Computations are performed in the complex domain to jointly capture amplitude and phase dynamics. Across three data partitions and three training seeds per partition, AM-PU-GRU achieves a mean mass interpolation RMSE of 0.2698 MeV and a temporal RMSE of 0.1905 MeV. Configuration comparisons support the integration of learned products into gated recurrence. Without mass inputs, MI-PU-GRU predicts charge radii with an RMSE of 0.019947 fm, 14.40% below the fixed WS* prior. Matched radius controls further show how candidate fusion shapes the benefit of learned products. Paired errors, nuclide-resolved maps, and inference measurements characterize predictive gains and computational cost. This article is an extended version of our ICCS 2026 conference paper (doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29921-5_10).

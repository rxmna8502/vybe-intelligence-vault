# DeGenTWeb: A First Look at MGT-dominant Websites

## Summary

arXiv:2605.00087v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Many news reports have claimed that machine-generated text (MGT) from large language models (LLMs) is taking over the web. However, these claims are typically not based on a representative sample of the web, and the methodology underlying them is often opaque. Consequently, we lack an understanding of the true prevalence and characteristics of MGT on the web.
 We describe DeGenTWeb, which systematically identifies MGT-dominant websites: sites whose informational prose content is mostly MGT, with little human input. We show how to adapt detectors of MGT for use on web pages, and how to aggregate detection results from multiple pages on a site for accurate site-level categorization. DeGenTWeb mislabels only 0.12% of human-authored sites as MGT-dominant. We find that 20.9% of the sites archived by Common Crawl between January and July 2026 and 13.6% of the sites linked from Bing's search results in 2025 are MGT-dominant. These figures are lower bounds on the prevalence of MGT-dominant sites. We also find that the fraction of MGT-dominant sites is growing over time, and that many such sites share the same owner. We will release all of our code and data after publication.

# PoCoFL: A Framework for POlicy-COmpliant Federated Learning

## Summary

arXiv:2610.03650v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Federated Learning (FL) is a privacy-oriented learning paradigm that enables collaborative model training while keeping training data local to clients. However, FL does not guarantee that clients submit policy-compliant contributions or that aggregators process admitted contributions correctly. Update-magnitude bounds alone cannot ensure compliance with learning-specific requirements involving private data, intermediate models, or retained state. These requirements differ across FL settings, motivating a unified framework to specify and verify client and aggregation policies. This work introduces PoCoFL, a policy-compliant federated learning framework that separates three aspects: (i) FL type, (ii) policy semantics, and (iii) cryptographic realisation. We provide a formalisation that captures client and aggregation requirements as policy-dependent relations, defining update admissibility, aggregation correctness, and policy-relative privacy. These relations enable verification of selected properties of intermediate objects, such as local-model loss, continual-learning state, and personalised optimisation conditions, in each round. Clients demonstrate compliance of their contributions using compatible cryptographic mechanisms, while aggregators prove that the recorded set of admitted contributions was processed according to the selected aggregation policy. We demonstrate PoCoFL through four formal instantiations and corresponding proof-of-concept realisations: (i) vanilla, (ii) continual, (iii) personalised, and (iv) threshold-encrypted federated learning. We evaluate how policy enforcement affects the learning objectives of vanilla, personalised, and continual FL, and quantify the computational and communication overhead of policy verification across all four instantiations. These results demonstrate that PoCoFL captures complex policy representations across the evaluated FL settings.

# Dual-Core Tensor Wheel Decomposition with Structure-Cached Acceleration for Tensor Recovery

## Summary

arXiv:2611.13938v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Tensor wheel decomposition captures complex correlations in high-dimensional data through interactions between outer-ring factors and a high-order central core. However, updating these components requires the explicit construction and manipulation of large-scale matrices, which limits computational efficiency and incurs particularly high computational and memory costs for the central-core update. To address these limitations, we propose dual-core tensor wheel decomposition (DCTW), which factorizes the central core into two lower-order cores linked by a shared latent bond. Each core is coupled to a different group of outer-ring factors. We further propose a structure-cached acceleration strategy that avoids explicit large-scale matrix multi- plications and caches intermediate tensors generated during tensor network contractions for reuse across block updates. This reuse reduces redundant computation and avoids the explicit construction of large matrices. Complexity analysis and experiments show that the strategy based on structural caching accelerates optimization compared with explicit and implicit up- date schemes while limiting the increase in peak memory as the ranks grow. Experiments on color video completion and robust denoising show that DCTW matches or outperforms representative tensor network methods in reconstruction quality, computational efficiency, and scalability.

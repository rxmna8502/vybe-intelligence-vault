# Poisson-GENERIC Neural Operators: Exact Metriplectic Structure in Function Space via Casimir Entropies

## Summary

arXiv:2610.05570v5 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Existing thermodynamically consistent neural operators enforce GENERIC degeneracy by projecting the reversible operator, introducing state dependence and generally forfeiting the Jacobi identity. We instead use the compatible Lie--Poisson pencil $L=\alpha D+\lambda(uD+Du)$ for nonlinear transport and constant Poisson multipliers otherwise. Augmenting $u$ with a latent entropy density $s$ makes $S=\int s$ a Casimir, so $L\,\delta S/\delta z=0$ without projection. The energy combines a fixed mechanical quadratic, a learned gauge-free potential, and a convex internal energy; the friction operator $M=AA^{\!\top}$ enforces $M\,\delta E/\delta z=0$ pointwise and supports diffusion and damping through local Onsager fluxes. Skewness, positivity, and both degeneracies hold algebraically for any parameters; the continuum pencil satisfies Jacobi, with discrete residuals at machine precision on alias-free inputs. Closed-form heat and damped-wave friction operators are exactly representable, and a discrete-gradient integrator preserves the first and second laws. Across four PDEs in one and two dimensions and three backbones (FNO, Transolver, CNO), the model wins $62/72$ seed-level comparisons against unconstrained baselines at equal optimizer steps, recovers exact transport and wave symbols, matches heat and Burgers dissipation within $12\%$, and dissipates nothing on advection. At equal wall-clock time, the unconstrained FNO is more accurate over ten frames. Over one hundred frames, in and out of distribution, our model never increases physical energy and conserves it when dissipation vanishes, whereas the unconstrained FNO loses a quarter of conserved energy out of distribution and projected GENERIC-FNO often creates energy. Limitations include late-time heat under-damping and explicit-integrator divergence on waves.

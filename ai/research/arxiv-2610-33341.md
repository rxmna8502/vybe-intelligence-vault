# When Are Nesterov Trajectories Eventually Locally Minimizing?

## Summary

arXiv:2610.33341v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We revisit the variational characterization of Nesterov's celebrated method and study when Nesterov trajectories are eventually locally minimizing in a variational sense, a notion that we define precisely. We show that this question is equivalent to the nonnegativity of an associated Schr\"odinger operator acting on variations of the trajectory. For power objectives of the form \[ f(x)=\frac{1}{p}\|x\|^p \] (where $\| \cdot\|$ denotes the Euclidean norm), we give a complete characterization: Among all exponents $p\ge 2$, the Nesterov trajectory with non-zero initial position is eventually locally minimizing if and only if $p=4$. We further interpret this exceptional exponent through the stability theory of semilinear elliptic equations.

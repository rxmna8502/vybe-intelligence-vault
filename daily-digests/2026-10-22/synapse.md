## Summary
Synapse is a production-ready RAG-based study assistant that enables users to upload documents (PDFs, DOCX, TXT, images) and interact with them via conversational chat, auto-generated quizzes, notes, and spaced-repetition flashcards. Every response is grounded in and cited to the source material for verifiability.

## Key Features
- Multi-format document ingestion with OCR support for scanned images and PDFs
- Hybrid retrieval combining dense semantic search (ChromaDB) and sparse BM25 keyword search
- Streaming chat with grounded citations and real-time source verification
- Automated generation of quizzes, notes, and SM-2 flashcards from uploaded material
- Built-in retrieval evaluation dashboard with precision, recall, MRR, and NDCG metrics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Synapse provides a robust, end-to-end RAG framework with production-grade engineering, making it essential for AI stack builders who need a citable, scalable, and feature-rich study assistant system.

## Summary

arXiv:2608.08463v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: In this short note, we revisit the classical Halpern iteration in 1967 and show its new applications in designing second- and higher-order methods for solving smooth monotone variational inequalities (MVI). By using a large-step inexact Halpern iteration, we propose a novel $p$th-order method Halpern-ATM, that achieves a fast convergence rate of $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}(T^{-p})$. This improves the best-known results for $p \ge 2$ since 2012.
